The Sony WH-1000XM4 is no longer Sony's newest flagship noise-canceling headphone. The WH-1000XM5 came after it, and the newer WH-1000XM6 now sits further up Sony's lineup.

So why are people still searching for the WH-1000XM4 in 2026?

Because the XM4 got many important things right: strong active noise cancellation, a comfortable and foldable design, useful smart features, LDAC support, multipoint Bluetooth, long battery life, and a sound profile that can be customized through Sony's app.

But there is an important question in 2026: Is the WH-1000XM4 still a smart buy, or should you spend more on a newer model?

Our Answer
The Sony WH-1000XM4 can still be an excellent purchase in 2026 — but only at the right price.

If you already own one and it still works well, there is usually no urgent reason to replace it. If you are buying one today, however, the price matters more than ever because newer headphones offer improvements in areas such as noise cancellation, microphone performance, connectivity, and other features.

1. Design and Build Quality

The WH-1000XM4 uses a closed-back, over-ear design with a predominantly lightweight plastic construction.

It does not have the luxurious metal construction of something like the AirPods Max. But that is not necessarily a disadvantage. A lighter headphone can be much easier to wear for several hours.

The XM4 also uses a foldable swivel design, which is one of the reasons it remains particularly attractive for travel. Sony designed the earcups to rotate inward so the headphones can fit into their included carrying case.

Is the design still modern in 2026? Not particularly. The XM4 looks recognizable as an older Sony flagship. Newer headphones can have cleaner or more modern-looking designs. But the XM4's design has an important practical advantage: it is built around portability rather than visual luxury. For travelers, that can matter more than premium-looking materials.

2. Comfort

Comfort is one of the XM4's strongest characteristics. Independent testing from RTINGS describes the headphones as comfortable and particularly suitable for travel and office use. Their closed-back construction does trap some heat, however, which makes them less ideal for intense exercise.

The large earcups and padded headband help distribute pressure. For long flights, office work, studying, or commuting, the XM4 makes considerably more sense than headphones designed primarily for sports.

But comfort is not perfect. No headphone fits every person identically. Some users may find the earcups too shallow or experience heat buildup during long sessions. The closed-back construction also means your ears can become warmer than they would with a more breathable design.

3. Active Noise Cancellation

This is still one of the main reasons to consider the WH-1000XM4. The XM4 uses Sony's noise-cancellation hardware and processing system together with microphones to analyze external sound and generate the appropriate cancellation signal.

Sony also includes: Adaptive Sound Control, Personal Noise Cancelling Optimizer, Atmospheric Pressure Optimizing, Ambient Sound Mode, and Quick Attention. These features are part of what makes the XM4 more than a basic Bluetooth headphone.

How good is the ANC in 2026? Still very good. Independent testing by RTINGS finds the XM4 particularly effective at reducing external noise across the frequency range, with especially strong performance against bass-heavy sounds such as airplane-engine rumble.

That makes the XM4 particularly attractive for: airplane travel, bus and train commuting, air conditioners, low-frequency machinery, and office background noise.

However, it is important not to exaggerate what ANC can do. ANC does not make the outside world disappear. Sudden sounds, voices, and higher-frequency noises can still be audible. And newer headphones have improved in some of these areas.

For example, RTINGS' current comparison between the XM4 and XM6 reports that the XM6 provides noticeably stronger ANC in the mid-range, particularly for background chatter in places such as cafes and open offices.

YallaBuy Verdict
ANC: Excellent for an older model. The XM4 is still a strong travel headphone, but it should not be marketed in 2026 as the absolute best ANC headphone available.

4. Sound Quality

The XM4 has never been designed around a strict studio-neutral sound. Its sound is better described as warm and bass-emphasized. Independent measurements from RTINGS characterize the XM4's sound signature as warm, with noticeably emphasized bass.

That can work extremely well for: hip-hop, EDM, pop, electronic music, movies, gaming, and casual listening. But listeners who prefer a highly neutral presentation may want something different.

The bass is one of the XM4's defining characteristics. It provides impact and warmth, but some listeners may find it excessive. That is where Sony's EQ becomes important.

The Sony app provides sound customization, allowing listeners to adjust the headphone's sound rather than being locked into the factory tuning. Sony also includes DSEE Extreme, which is designed to process compressed music and restore some high-frequency information.

Can EQ improve the XM4? Yes. This is one of the headphone's biggest advantages. Instead of judging the XM4 only from its factory tuning, users can adjust the sound according to their preferences. A listener who finds the bass too strong can reduce it. Someone who wants more presence in vocals can increase the appropriate frequencies. That flexibility gives the XM4 more longevity than a headphone with a fixed sound signature.

5. LDAC and High-Resolution Bluetooth

One of the XM4's important advantages is LDAC. Sony's official specifications list SBC, AAC, and LDAC as supported Bluetooth audio formats. Sony states that LDAC can transmit up to 990 kbps and is designed to carry considerably more audio data than conventional Bluetooth audio.

However, there is an important distinction: higher bitrate does not automatically mean that every listener will hear a dramatic improvement. Your source, device, settings, environment, and listening material all matter.

Android users with LDAC-compatible devices can take advantage of this feature. iPhone users do not get LDAC support, so the XM4's codec advantage is different on Apple's platform.

6. Microphone and Call Quality

This is an area where we need to correct a common misconception. The XM4 has multiple microphones and Sony's Precise Voice Pickup Technology. Sony describes the system as using five microphones together with signal processing to improve voice pickup.

But having five microphones does not automatically mean the XM4 has excellent call quality. Independent testing tells a more nuanced story.

RTINGS currently describes the XM4's microphone performance as disappointing, noting that voices can sound thin and muffled and that background-noise isolation is not especially strong. That is an important consideration.

For occasional calls, the XM4 is usable. For constant professional calls, we would not choose it primarily because of its microphone. If microphone quality is one of your highest priorities, newer headphones such as the XM6 have a clear advantage according to current independent testing.

YallaBuy Verdict
Microphone: Average to good for everyday use, but not a class-leading reason to buy the XM4.

7. Battery Life

Battery life remains one of the XM4's strengths. Sony officially rates it for up to 30 hours with noise cancellation ON and up to 38 hours with noise cancellation OFF. Sony lists approximately three hours for a full charge.

Independent testing has also found battery endurance to be strong, with RTINGS reporting more than 37 hours of continuous battery life in its testing conditions.

Of course, actual battery life varies with: volume, codec, ANC, Ambient Sound, calling, DSEE Extreme, other settings, and battery age.

Battery age matters in 2026

This is especially important if you buy an older or refurbished XM4. A brand-new XM4 and a heavily used XM4 from 2020 are not necessarily going to deliver the same battery endurance. Lithium-ion batteries naturally lose capacity over time. So when buying a used or renewed XM4, battery condition should be part of your decision.

8. Smart Features

The XM4 was unusually feature-rich when it launched, and many of those features remain useful.

Wearing Detection

The proximity sensor can detect when the headphones are removed and automatically pause playback. Sony officially describes this feature as part of the XM4's smart functionality.

Speak-to-Chat

Speak-to-Chat detects when you begin talking and can automatically pause or reduce your music while allowing ambient sound through. Sony states that the default resume time is approximately 30 seconds after the conversation ends, and this timing can be customized in the app. The feature is useful, but not everyone likes it. Some users can trigger it unintentionally when speaking, singing, coughing, or making other sounds. Sony even provides troubleshooting guidance for cases where Speak-to-Chat activates unexpectedly. Fortunately, the feature can be disabled.

Quick Attention

Place your hand over the appropriate earcup and the headphone temporarily reduces music volume and allows ambient sound through. This is useful when you need to hear an announcement or quickly speak to someone without removing the headphones.

9. Multipoint Bluetooth

Multipoint is one of the XM4's best practical features. The headphones can connect to two Bluetooth devices simultaneously. For example: laptop + smartphone. You can listen to music from your laptop while remaining connected to your phone. When a call arrives, you can switch to the phone and then return to the computer afterward.

Sony's current support documentation confirms that the XM4 supports simultaneous connection to two Bluetooth devices. There is one limitation: two devices, not an unlimited number of active connections. For students and office workers, this remains a very useful feature.

10. Sony's Companion App

The software experience is another reason the XM4 has aged well. Sony's current app ecosystem allows users to control and customize headphone features. Depending on the feature and software version, users can configure things such as: equalizer, ambient sound, ANC behavior, touch controls, Speak-to-Chat, multipoint, and other device settings.

Sony continues to provide support resources for the XM4, including firmware and software information. Sony's support page currently lists firmware version 3.0.1 released in October 2025 and the Sony | Sound Connect app updates in 2026. That continued support is useful for owners in 2026.

11. Connectivity

The XM4 uses Bluetooth 5.0. Supported Bluetooth audio codecs are SBC, AAC, and LDAC. Sony lists an effective Bluetooth range of approximately 10 meters / 30 feet under line-of-sight conditions. The headphones also include a 3.5mm connection and support passive operation. This gives the XM4 flexibility that some newer wireless headphones do not prioritize.

12. Travel Performance

If you travel frequently, the XM4 remains extremely compelling. You get: strong ANC, foldable design, carrying case, long battery life, comfortable fit, Quick Attention, Ambient Sound, wired fallback, and multipoint.

RTINGS specifically recommends the XM4 for travel and commuting, citing its comfort, ANC, carrying case, and battery performance. The XM4 also includes atmospheric-pressure optimization, a feature Sony designed with air travel in mind.

Travel Score

13. Office and Study Performance

For studying or office work, the XM4 makes a lot of sense. Its ANC can reduce distracting background noise, while multipoint lets you remain connected to both your computer and phone. RTINGS similarly rates the XM4 highly for office use, while noting that some audio leakage can occur at high listening volumes.

One warning

ANC should not be treated as a substitute for situational awareness. When walking near traffic or in other potentially dangerous environments, use ambient mode or remove the headphones when appropriate.

14. Gaming

The XM4 can work for wired gaming because it has a 3.5mm connection. However, wireless gaming is not its strongest use case. RTINGS reports that Bluetooth latency can be too high for gaming where synchronization between sound and video matters, and the headphone does not provide the same console-focused wireless compatibility as dedicated gaming headsets.

YallaBuy Recommendation
For music + occasional gaming: Fine. For competitive gaming: Choose a dedicated gaming headset.

But the XM4 still has an important practical advantage: the XM4 folds. The XM4 also remains attractive when its price is significantly lower.

Choose XM4 if:

  • It is considerably cheaper
  • You prefer the folding design
  • You want strong ANC and LDAC without paying for the newest generation

Choose XM5 if:

19. What About the AirPods Max?

The AirPods Max is a very different product philosophy. Apple emphasizes premium materials and deep integration with its ecosystem. The XM4 is lighter, more compact, and much more focused on traditional Bluetooth flexibility.

For iPhone users who are deeply invested in Apple's ecosystem, AirPods Max can have advantages. But if your priorities are: lower weight, foldability, LDAC on compatible devices, broader Bluetooth flexibility, and lower potential cost — the XM4 is a much more practical design.

21. Is the Sony WH-1000XM4 Still Worth Buying in 2026?

Yes — with one major condition.

The price must be right. That is the central point of this entire review.

The XM4 was originally a premium flagship. Today, it is an older product competing against newer headphones. That changes the buying equation.

You should not ask: "Was the XM4 one of the best headphones when it launched?" The answer to that historical question does not determine whether it is a good purchase today.

Instead, ask: "What am I paying for the XM4 compared with the newer alternatives available today?"

That is the correct 2026 buying question.

22. Should Existing XM4 Owners Upgrade?

For most owners: No.

If your XM4 still has good battery life, still fits comfortably, has working ANC, has healthy ear pads, and sounds good to you — there is no technological emergency requiring an upgrade. The XM4 still provides the core experience many people actually want: wireless audio + ANC + comfort + battery + smart features.

Upgrade to XM6 if: you want better ANC, you make lots of calls, you want newer features, you want the latest Sony platform, and you can afford the price difference.

Keep the XM4 if: you are happy with its sound, ANC is already good enough, battery life is still healthy, you mainly use it for travel and studying, and the XM6 would be an expensive upgrade for relatively small personal benefit.

24. Buying Used or Renewed in 2026

This is where buyers need to be careful. Because the XM4 is an older generation, used and refurbished examples can be attractive. But condition matters.

Before buying a used unit, check:

  • Battery: Ask whether the battery still provides reasonable playback time.
  • Ear Pads: Check for peeling, cracking, flattening, or excessive wear.
  • Hinges: Open and close both sides carefully.
  • ANC: Make sure ANC and Ambient Sound work correctly.
  • Touch controls: Test volume, playback, and Quick Attention.
  • Sensors: Check whether removing the headphones pauses playback as expected.
  • Bluetooth: Test pairing and multipoint if that feature is important to you.
  • Seller: Pay attention to seller reputation, return policy, warranty, product condition, and authenticity.
Important

A cheap XM4 is not automatically a good deal if the battery is badly degraded or the headphones are counterfeit.

27. Final Verdict

Sony WH-1000XM4
Still Good, But No Longer the Automatic Choice

The Sony WH-1000XM4 has aged surprisingly well. Its strongest characteristics are still relevant: excellent ANC, comfort, battery life, LDAC, multipoint, portability, and extensive software features. Independent testing confirms that the XM4 remains strong for travel and office use and provides excellent noise isolation.

But it is important to recognize where newer headphones have moved ahead. The XM6 offers stronger noise cancellation in some important frequency ranges and better microphone performance according to current independent comparisons.

That means the XM4's biggest advantage in 2026 is no longer being "the best." Its biggest advantage is: Value.

If you can get the XM4 at a substantially lower price than a newer flagship, it can still be an excellent purchase. If the price difference is small, the newer model becomes much easier to recommend.
