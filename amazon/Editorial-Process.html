Editorial Process

How We Create and Review Our Content

At YallaBuy, our editorial process exists to help readers make better buying decisions using accurate and useful information. This page explains exactly how that works.

Accuracy First

Every piece of information is checked against reliable sources before publication.

Reader First

Your needs drive every decision — not sales, not commissions, not trends.

Transparent Recommendations

We clearly disclose how we choose products and how we make money.

Regular Updates

Content is reviewed and updated when important product information changes.

Our Process

Our Editorial Process

Every article on YallaBuy goes through a structured process — from identifying a real product need to publishing and maintaining accurate content over time.

1

Identifying a Real Product Need

YallaBuy focuses on products that can solve a specific problem or meet a specific user need. Products are not selected simply because they are popular or because they provide affiliate commissions.

The goal is always the same: help readers solve a real problem and make a better purchasing decision.

Products may be researched with the help of AI during the research and organization process, but AI does not replace human editorial judgment. The decision to cover a product is always made by the writer.

2

Product Research

YallaBuy researches products using reliable sources. These include:

  • Amazon
  • Manufacturer websites
  • Reliable independent sources
  • Product information and specifications
  • User feedback when relevant

In some cases, YallaBuy may use Google Trends and other research methods to understand what people are searching for and what topics may be useful to readers. This is not a required step for every article — it depends on the topic and the information needed.

3

Verifying Information

Accuracy is one of the most important parts of the editorial process. Important product information is checked against multiple sources whenever possible.

If two sources provide conflicting information, YallaBuy investigates the subject more deeply instead of simply choosing one source. The goal is to find the most accurate information available.

This may include checking manufacturer information, Amazon information, and reliable independent sources to resolve any inconsistencies.

4

Product Analysis

Before recommending a product, YallaBuy analyzes it based on several important factors:

  • Performance
  • Price
  • Features
  • Reliability
  • Value for money

Different products may be better for different users. YallaBuy does not claim that one product is automatically the best for everyone.

When choosing products such as "Best Overall", "Best Budget", or similar recommendations, the recommendation is based on the product's usefulness, features, performance, price, reliability, and overall value — not on which product pays the highest commission.

5

Ratings and User Feedback

YallaBuy may display product ratings from Amazon on its pages. It is important to understand that these ratings represent ratings from Amazon customers and should not be presented as YallaBuy's own testing or rating.

YallaBuy does not imply that it personally tested products unless the article explicitly states so. Customer ratings are one of many data points considered, not YallaBuy's own evaluation.

6

Writing the Content

The final editorial content on YallaBuy is written by Mahmoud Hamdy, the site's founder and writer.

AI may assist with certain tasks during the content creation process, including:

  • Research
  • Organizing information
  • Improving keyword selection
  • Finding suitable keywords

However, AI is not treated as the final authority for factual information. All important information is reviewed before publication. AI assistance does not replace human editorial judgment — it supports it.

7

Pre-Publishing Review

Before any article is published, YallaBuy reviews the content thoroughly. The content is reviewed by the site's founder and writer — not a large editorial team.

The review covers the following areas:

Accuracy of information
Language and writing errors
Links
Product prices
Product specifications
SEO
Images
Schema markup
8

SEO and Search Intent

SEO is part of the editorial process at YallaBuy. The site sometimes performs keyword research and considers search intent when planning content.

However, SEO is used to support the reader — not to make the content difficult to read or to manipulate search rankings. The primary goal is always:

"Helping readers make the right purchasing decision."

YallaBuy does not make unrealistic claims about search rankings, guaranteed traffic, or guaranteed sales.

9

Prices and Availability

Product prices are recorded based on the price available when the article is written or updated. However, prices can change every day on retail platforms like Amazon.

Product availability can also change at any time.

Transparency notice: The price shown in an article may no longer match the current price at the time you read it. YallaBuy does not control Amazon prices or product availability. Always check the current price on the retailer's website before purchasing.

10

Affiliate Transparency

YallaBuy uses affiliate links, including Amazon affiliate links.

YallaBuy may earn a commission when readers purchase products through affiliate links.

This is important to understand: affiliate commissions do not determine which products YallaBuy recommends. The primary consideration is always the product's usefulness and value to the reader.

YallaBuy does not claim that it has tested products when it has not. For full details, you can read our Affiliate Disclosure page.

11

Product Testing Transparency

This is one of the most important sections on this page.

YallaBuy does not personally test every product.

Unless an article explicitly states that YallaBuy physically tested, measured, reviewed, or benchmarked a product, you should assume it was not. YallaBuy clearly distinguishes between:

  • Manufacturer claims
  • Amazon customer ratings
  • Independent testing by third parties
  • User feedback
  • YallaBuy's own analysis

YallaBuy never fabricates test results, battery tests, performance measurements, lab results, personal experiences, hands-on testing, or ratings. Accuracy and transparency always come before making a product look attractive.

12

Images and Visual Content

YallaBuy may use product images from appropriate sources. In some cases, product images may be enhanced for:

  • Quality
  • Clarity
  • Presentation
  • Visual consistency

When an image is enhanced, the actual appearance and design of the product is not intentionally changed.

YallaBuy may also use AI-generated visuals for comparison graphics or other visual content. YallaBuy does not claim that every image on the website is original photography, and does not claim that products were personally photographed unless explicitly stated.

13

Publishing

After the content passes the editorial review, it can be published. But publishing is not necessarily the end of the process.

Content can be reviewed and updated later when important information changes. YallaBuy treats published content as a living resource, not a one-time publication.

14

Content Updates

YallaBuy updates content when important information changes. This includes situations such as:

  • Product specifications change
  • A newer product replaces an older product
  • A product becomes outdated
  • Important product information changes
  • Product availability changes
  • Other important information becomes outdated

When an older product is no longer a good recommendation, YallaBuy may replace it with a newer or more relevant product.

Each article displays a "Last Updated" date when applicable. The information in an article represents what was available at the time of the latest update.

Product Selection

How We Choose Products

YallaBuy chooses products based on usefulness and reader value — not on affiliate commissions or popularity alone.

When evaluating products for recommendation, YallaBuy considers several factors:

Performance
Price
Features
Reliability
Value for money

Affiliate commissions do not determine our recommendations. The reader's needs come first. A product that pays a higher commission will not be recommended over a better product for the reader.

Verification

How We Verify Information

Accuracy is at the core of everything YallaBuy publishes. Here is how information is verified before it reaches you.

Important product information is checked against multiple sources whenever possible. YallaBuy does not rely on a single source for critical product details.

The verification process may include:

  • Cross-checking manufacturer specifications with retailer listings
  • Comparing information from multiple independent sources
  • Reviewing customer feedback for common inconsistencies
  • Investigating conflicts between sources rather than picking one at random

If two sources provide conflicting information, YallaBuy investigates the subject more deeply instead of simply choosing one source. The goal is always to find the most accurate information available — even if it takes more time.

Analysis

How We Analyze Products

YallaBuy analyzes products based on multiple factors to provide useful recommendations for different types of users.

Each product is evaluated based on:

  • Performance — How well does the product do what it's designed to do?
  • Price — Is the price fair for what the product offers?
  • Features — Does it include the features that matter most to users?
  • Reliability — Is the product from a reputable brand? Do users report issues?
  • Value for money — Does the overall package justify the cost?

When YallaBuy labels a product as "Best Overall", "Best Budget", or similar, that recommendation is based on the combination of these factors — not on a single metric, and not on affiliate commission rates.

Different products may be better for different users. YallaBuy does not guarantee that a recommended product is the right choice for every person.

AI Transparency

Our Use of AI

YallaBuy is transparent about how AI is used in the content creation process.

AI may assist with certain tasks, including:

  • Research and information gathering
  • Organizing information and structuring content
  • Keyword selection and content preparation

However, there are important boundaries:

  • AI is not treated as a final factual authority
  • All important information is reviewed by the human writer
  • Final editorial decisions are made by the human writer
  • AI does not replace human editorial judgment

AI is a tool that supports the editorial process — it does not run it. The content you read on YallaBuy is shaped by human judgment, not automated generation.

Testing

Product Testing Transparency

This section exists because honesty about testing is essential to trust.

YallaBuy does not personally test every product.

Unless an article explicitly states that YallaBuy physically tested, measured, reviewed, or benchmarked a product, you should assume it was not tested by YallaBuy.

YallaBuy clearly distinguishes between different types of information:

  • Manufacturer claims — What the brand says about its product
  • Amazon customer ratings — What Amazon customers have rated the product

  • User feedback — What real users have reported about their experience

YallaBuy never fabricates:

  • Test results
  • Battery tests
  • Performance measurements
  • Lab results
  • Personal experiences
  • Hands-on testing claims
  • Ratings

Accuracy and transparency come before making a product look attractive. If YallaBuy hasn't tested a product, it won't say it has.

Values
