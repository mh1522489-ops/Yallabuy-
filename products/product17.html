Feature Deep Dive

01

Crisp and Balanced Sound

Sound quality is the soul of any speaker. The SoundLink Flex delivers the kind of crisp, balanced audio you'd expect from Bose — punchy mids, controlled bass, and clean vocals. Whether it's music, podcasts, or audiobooks, the listening experience stays consistent at low and moderate volumes.

The company's decades of audio engineering show in how the speaker handles different genres. It's not the loudest in its class, but it is among the most refined.

02

PositionIQ Technology

Portable speakers end up in all sorts of places — standing up on a shelf, lying flat on a table, or hanging from a carabiner. PositionIQ detects the orientation and automatically adjusts the audio profile so the sound stays full and balanced.

You don't have to think about it. Just place the speaker and let the tech do its thing.

03

Waterproof & Dustproof (IP67)

The IP67 rating means the SoundLink Flex is fully sealed against dust and can survive temporary water immersion. The "6" is the highest dust-protection level, and the "7" means it handles being dunked in up to 1 meter of water for 30 minutes.

Perfect for poolside, beach days, camping, hiking, or just a rainy backyard hangout.

04

Up to 12 Hours of Battery

Bose claims up to 12 hours of playback per charge. Real-world use lands somewhere between 9–12 hours depending on volume, content type, and whether you use voice prompts. More than enough for a full day out or a weekend at the cabin.

Charging is via USB-C — fast and convenient with the same cable most of your other gadgets use.

05

Portable Design for Everyday Use

Compact, lightweight, and built with a utility loop so you can clip it to a bag. The silicone exterior is soft to the touch but tough enough to survive the bumps of daily life.

It's the kind of speaker that disappears into your routine — bedroom in the morning, kitchen during the day, balcony in the evening.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Bose SoundLink Flex waterproof?

What is PositionIQ technology?

It automatically detects the speaker's orientation (upright, flat, or hanging) and adjusts the audio output for that placement.
