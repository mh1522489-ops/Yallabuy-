Buying

AirPods Pro 3 are worth buying if you are invested in the Apple ecosystem and want a reliable pair of true wireless earbuds with strong noise cancellation, good sound quality, and seamless device switching. They are not the cheapest option, but the combination of features and integration makes them a solid choice for iPhone users.
AirPods Pro 3 bring an updated H3 chip, improved Active Noise Cancellation, a new IP57 dust and water resistance rating for the earbuds, an updated in-ear design for better comfort, heart-rate sensing capabilities, and enhanced hearing health features. The charging case also receives a refreshed design.
AirPods Pro 3 offer meaningful upgrades over Pro 2, including better noise cancellation, an upgraded IP57 rating compared to IP54, the addition of heart-rate tracking, and improved hearing features. If you already own AirPods Pro 2 and are satisfied with them, the upgrade is not essential but is noticeable.
The most significant upgrades are the improved Active Noise Cancellation and the new IP57 rating, which adds meaningful dust resistance and better water protection compared to the previous generation. The heart-rate sensor is also a notable new addition.
AirPods Pro 3 are priced at $249 in the US at launch. Prices on Amazon may vary depending on sales, promotions, or third-party sellers, so it is worth checking the current price before purchasing.
AirPods Pro 3 are best suited for iPhone users who want strong noise cancellation, a comfortable in-ear fit, water resistance for workouts, and seamless integration across Apple devices. If you primarily use Android or need high-resolution audio codecs, other options may be more appropriate.

Sound & ANC

Yes, AirPods Pro 3 deliver good sound quality with a balanced profile that works well across most music genres. They are not audiophile-grade earbuds, but for everyday listening, podcasts, and calls, the sound is clear and detailed.
The bass is punchy and well-controlled, which is typical for Apple's in-ear earbuds. It is not overpowering but provides a satisfying low-end response for most music styles. If you prefer heavy bass, you may want to adjust EQ settings in your device.
The Active Noise Cancellation on AirPods Pro 3 is improved over the previous generation and performs well at reducing low-frequency noise like engine hum and air conditioning. It is competitive with other premium true wireless earbuds on the market.
Yes, the combination of strong ANC and a good in-ear seal makes AirPods Pro 3 effective in noisy environments such as offices, public transport, and flights. They handle consistent background noise very well.
Yes, AirPods Pro 3 support Adaptive Audio, which automatically adjusts the balance between noise cancellation and transparency mode based on your environment. This feature requires an Apple device running a compatible iOS version.
Yes, Transparency Mode lets outside sound in so you can hear your surroundings. It is useful for conversations or situations where you need situational awareness. The implementation remains one of the more natural-sounding transparency modes available.
Yes, AirPods Pro 3 support Spatial Audio with dynamic head tracking, which creates a surround-sound-like experience for supported content. This works with Apple TV+ content, some music in Apple Music, and other supported apps.
No, AirPods Pro 3 do not support true lossless audio over Bluetooth. Bluetooth bandwidth limitations prevent lossless transmission. Apple uses its own codec (SBC and AAC) which provides good quality but is not lossless.
AirPods Pro 3 support the SBC codec universally and the AAC codec, which is used by default with Apple devices. They do not support aptX, LDAC, or other high-resolution Bluetooth codecs.

Battery & Charging

AirPods Pro 3 offer up to 6 hours of listening time with Active Noise Cancellation enabled, and up to 30 hours of total listening time when using the charging case. Battery life varies based on volume level, content type, and feature usage.
The charging case provides multiple additional charges, bringing total listening time up to about 30 hours with ANC on. The case itself holds enough charge for roughly 4-5 full earbud recharges.
A 5-minute charge in the case provides approximately 1 hour of listening time. A full charge of the earbuds takes around 30-45 minutes depending on the charging method used.
Yes, the AirPods Pro 3 charging case uses a USB-C port for wired charging, which is the same connector used by current iPhones and many modern devices.
Yes, the charging case supports Qi wireless charging and is also compatible with MagSafe chargers for aligned wireless charging with Apple devices.

Comfort & Workout

Most users find AirPods Pro 3 comfortable for extended listening, thanks to the updated in-ear design with softer ear tips. However, comfort is subjective and depends on your ear shape. Apple includes multiple ear tip sizes to help with fit.
Generally yes, the in-ear design with silicone tips provides a secure fit for most users during moderate physical activity. Using the correct ear tip size is important for both comfort and security during movement.
Yes, AirPods Pro 3 work well for running. Their lightweight design, secure in-ear fit, and water resistance make them suitable for outdoor running in various weather conditions.
AirPods Pro 3 are suitable for most workouts including gym sessions, cardio, and weight training. Their sweat resistance, secure fit, and good sound quality make them a practical choice for fitness use.

Water Resistance

No, AirPods Pro 3 are not waterproof. They are water resistant with an IP57 rating for the earbuds, which means they can handle sweat and rain but are not designed for submersion in water. Water resistant is not the same as waterproof.
No, you should not swim with AirPods Pro 3. Despite the IP57 rating, Apple does not describe them as suitable for swimming. Water resistance is intended for sweat and rain protection, not submersion or high-pressure water exposure.
IP57 means the earbuds are dust-tight (5) and can withstand immersion in water up to 1 meter deep for up to 30 minutes (7). However, this rating is tested under controlled laboratory conditions and does not guarantee performance in real-world swimming or high-pressure water scenarios.

Compatibility

Yes, AirPods Pro 3 work as standard Bluetooth earbuds with Android phones. You can listen to music, take calls, and use basic controls. However, some Apple-specific features are not available on Android.
Yes, AirPods Pro 3 connect to Windows PCs via Bluetooth and work for audio playback, video calls, and meetings. You will not have access to Apple-specific features, but basic functionality works well.
AirPods Pro 3 are compatible with iPhones running iOS 18 or later. For the full set of features, you need a relatively recent iPhone model. Basic Bluetooth audio works with older iPhones, but some features may be limited.
No. Features like Adaptive Audio, personalized Spatial Audio, automatic device switching, Find My integration, and heart-rate tracking require Apple devices. On Android, you get audio playback, ANC, transparency mode, and call functionality.

Calls & Smart Features

The microphone quality is good for a true wireless earbud, with clear voice pickup in most environments. The beamforming microphones help reduce background noise during calls, though performance can decrease in very windy or noisy conditions.
Yes, AirPods Pro 3 are well-suited for phone calls and video meetings. The combination of good microphones, noise cancellation, and transparency mode makes them reliable for voice communication in various environments.
Yes, AirPods Pro 3 include a heart-rate sensor. This feature integrates with Apple Health and can provide heart-rate data during workouts and throughout the day without needing a separate device like an Apple Watch.
Yes, AirPods Pro 3 support enhanced hearing features including a clinical-grade hearing aid functionality for users with mild to moderate hearing loss, available through a compatible iPhone running iOS 18 or later. This requires a professional hearing test setup through the Health app.
Yes, AirPods Pro 3 can work with the Live Translation feature introduced in iOS 18, which translates conversations in real time through the earbuds when using a compatible iPhone.

Comparisons

AirPods Pro 3 are adequate for casual mobile gaming but are not ideal for competitive gaming. Bluetooth introduces some audio latency, which may be noticeable in fast-paced games. For serious gaming, wired or dedicated low-latency wireless earbuds are generally better.
AirPods Pro 3 offer better integration with Apple devices and a more refined transparency mode. Galaxy Buds4 Pro support high-resolution codecs like SSC HiFi and 24-bit audio, which AirPods lack. Galaxy Buds4 Pro are better for Android users, while AirPods Pro 3 are the clear choice for iPhone users. Both have strong ANC and good sound quality.

Should You Buy AirPods Pro 3?

A balanced conclusion based on who benefits most.

Buy If
  • You use an iPhone or other Apple devices daily
  • You want strong, reliable Active Noise Cancellation
  • You value seamless ecosystem integration and switching
  • Water resistance for workouts is important to you
  • You are interested in hearing health features
Think Twice If
  • You primarily use an Android phone
  • You want maximum battery life beyond 6 hours per charge
  • You need high-resolution audio codecs like LDAC or aptX
  • You are on a strict budget under $150
  • You already own AirPods Pro 2 and are fully satisfied
Best For
  • iPhone users upgrading from wired EarPods or older AirPods
  • People who want one earbud pair for everything: music, calls, workouts
  • Users who value simplicity and "just works" reliability
  • Those interested in heart-rate tracking without wearing a watch
