Frequently asked questions

What will I receive?

You'll receive YallaBuy updates such as buying advice, product insights, comparisons, guides, and other useful content. The exact frequency and content may vary.

How often will you email me?

YallaBuy prioritizes usefulness over sending emails simply for the sake of frequency. You'll hear from us when there's something genuinely worth sharing, not on a fixed promotional schedule.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The newsletter is designed to work without a YallaBuy account or membership.

Will you sell my email address?

We do not sell your email address to third parties. Your information is used solely to send you the YallaBuy newsletter, in line with our privacy practices.

Can I unsubscribe later?

Yes. Every newsletter email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can opt out at any time.

Is everything in the newsletter personally tested by YallaBuy?

Content is based on research, manufacturer information, independent sources, and analysis, with hands-on testing noted when it applies. We're clear about which is which so you always know where the information comes from.
