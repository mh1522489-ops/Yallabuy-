AirPods Pro 3 Review: 10 Things to Know Before Buying

The AirPods Pro 3 are Apple's flagship wireless earbuds, combining strong noise cancellation with new health and fitness features, improved water resistance, and deeper integration with the Apple ecosystem.

ByMahmoud Hamdy
August 11, 2026

Quick Verdict

Independent testing from outlets such as SoundGuys and RTINGS has placed the AirPods Pro 3 among the strongest noise-cancelling earbuds tested, making ANC one of their biggest selling points.

The earbuds also introduce heart-rate sensing for workouts, Live Translation, expanded hearing health features, and an IP57 rating.

The main drawback is that they are still heavily optimized for Apple devices. Android users can use them for basic Bluetooth audio and calls, but many of the features that make the AirPods Pro 3 special are limited or unavailable outside Apple's ecosystem.

There have also been recurring owner reports of static or hissing sounds in ANC, Adaptive, and Transparency modes, as well as mixed feedback about the redesigned ear tips.

1. Noise Cancellation Is One of the AirPods Pro 3's Biggest Advantages

If there's one feature that stands out most, it's noise cancellation.

Apple claims the AirPods Pro 3 can provide up to twice the noise cancellation of the AirPods Pro 2 and up to four times that of the original AirPods Pro under Apple's testing conditions.

More importantly, independent testing has also produced very strong results.

SoundGuys measured approximately 90% average noise attenuation in its testing and placed the AirPods Pro 3 among the strongest noise-cancelling earbuds it has tested.

RTINGS also reported very strong noise reduction across important frequency ranges.

However, it's better not to treat the AirPods Pro 3 as universally and objectively "the best ANC earbuds." Audio testing depends on equipment, ear shape, fit, frequency range, and methodology.

The AirPods Pro 3 offer class-leading ANC performance and rank among the strongest noise-cancelling earbuds in independent testing.

That's still an impressive result.

2. Sound Quality Has Improved, but Firmware Can Change It

The AirPods Pro 3 use a new acoustic design intended to improve soundstage, bass, and vocal clarity.

Independent listening tests have generally described the earbuds as delivering strong overall sound quality, with satisfying bass and clear detail.

Spatial Audio is another important part of the experience, particularly for Apple users who watch movies, TV shows, and other compatible content.

One interesting point is that firmware updates can change how wireless earbuds sound.

Testing from SoundGuys and RTINGS detected changes in the AirPods Pro 3's frequency response following a firmware update.

That means the sound you get today may not be exactly the same as the sound you get several months later.

For most users, this isn't a major problem, but it's worth remembering when comparing different reviews.

3. Battery Life Is Better, but It Isn't a Class Leader

Apple rates the AirPods Pro 3 for up to 8 hours of listening time with ANC enabled.

With certain Transparency and Hearing Aid use cases, Apple reports up to 10 hours.

That's an improvement, but battery life isn't necessarily the strongest reason to choose these earbuds.

Several reviewers have described the battery performance as adequate rather than exceptional, especially when features such as ANC, Spatial Audio, and heart-rate sensing are being used together.

If you regularly spend an entire day away from a charger, compare battery performance carefully against competing earbuds.

For normal daily use, however, the charging case makes the overall battery experience much more practical.

4. There Are Reports of Static and Hissing

This is one of the most important issues to know before buying.

Some AirPods Pro 3 owners have reported hearing:

  • Static
  • Hissing
  • White noise
  • Background noise
  • Unwanted sounds when no media is playing

Reports have appeared particularly around ANC, Adaptive, and Transparency modes.

The important distinction is that this does not appear to affect every AirPods Pro 3 unit.

Some owners report completely normal operation, while others describe the noise as distracting.

Because the issue has been reported by multiple owners and covered by technology publications, it deserves attention.

However, it should not automatically be interpreted as proof that every AirPods Pro 3 has a hardware defect.

Firmware updates can potentially affect these issues, so buyers should check the latest owner reports and firmware information before purchasing.

If you are especially sensitive to background hiss, buying from a retailer with an easy return policy is a smart precaution.

5. Heart Rate Sensing Makes the AirPods Pro 3 More Useful for Fitness

One of the biggest new features is heart-rate sensing.

The AirPods Pro 3 can measure heart rate during workouts and integrate the data with Apple's Fitness ecosystem.

This makes them more interesting for people who exercise regularly.

For example, runners and gym users can potentially use the earbuds to collect workout-related heart-rate information without relying entirely on a smartwatch.

However, this should not be confused with a medical-grade heart-rate monitor.

Independent testing of heart-rate accuracy is more limited than testing of audio and ANC performance.

Therefore, it's better to think of the feature as a convenient fitness tool rather than a replacement for a dedicated medical or fitness device.

6. Live Translation Is Useful, but It Requires Apple's Ecosystem

Live Translation is another major addition.

The feature can help translate conversations in real time when used with compatible Apple hardware and software.

Supported languages and availability depend on Apple's current software and regional support.

The important thing to understand is that Live Translation isn't simply a feature that works independently from the earbuds.

You need a compatible Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone and supported software.

So if translation is the main reason you're considering the AirPods Pro 3, check your iPhone model, iOS version, region, and supported languages before purchasing.

7. The Fit Has Changed

Apple redesigned the AirPods Pro 3 fit and ear tips, using research and extensive ear-scan data to improve stability and sealing.

Independent testing has generally found the fit to be secure.

However, owner experiences are mixed. Some users prefer the new tips, while others report:

  • Ear discomfort
  • Irritation
  • Pressure
  • Difficulty maintaining a seal
  • Earbuds falling out
  • Preference for the AirPods Pro 2 fit

This is one area where no specification sheet can tell you exactly what will happen. Ear shape varies considerably from person to person.

If the AirPods Pro 2 never fit you comfortably, don't assume the AirPods Pro 3 will automatically solve the problem.

If possible, try them in person or purchase from a retailer with a straightforward return policy.

8. IP57 Is a Major Durability Upgrade

The AirPods Pro 3 have an IP57 rating. That's an important upgrade compared with the IPX4 rating of previous AirPods Pro models.

The improved rating provides better protection against dust and water exposure and makes the earbuds more suitable for activities such as:

  • Running
  • Gym workouts
  • HIIT
  • Outdoor exercise
  • Sweaty workouts
  • Rainy conditions

However, water-resistant does not mean waterproof. You should not treat the AirPods Pro 3 as swimming headphones or intentionally submerge them.

9. Android Compatibility Works, but It's Limited

Yes, you can connect AirPods Pro 3 to an Android smartphone using Bluetooth.

You can use them for basic functions such as:

  • Listening to music
  • Watching videos
  • Making calls

But that's where the major difference appears. Many of the features that make AirPods Pro 3 attractive are designed around Apple's ecosystem.

Depending on your device and software, Android users may lose access to or have limited access to features such as:

  • Live Translation
  • Apple Fitness integration
  • Seamless Apple device switching
  • Find My integration
  • Advanced Apple ecosystem features
  • Some configuration options

If you're an Android user, alternatives such as Samsung Galaxy Buds4 Pro, Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds, or Sony's flagship earbuds may provide better overall functionality.

10. Hearing Health Features Are More Than a Gimmick

The AirPods Pro 3 include several hearing-related features, including:

  • Hearing Test
  • Hearing Aid functionality
  • Hearing Protection

These features make the AirPods Pro 3 different from many competing wireless earbuds.

Apple positions some of these capabilities around users with perceived mild-to-moderate hearing loss, but availability can depend on regulatory approval and region.

That means you should not assume every hearing-related feature is available in every country.

If hearing assistance is one of your primary reasons for buying the AirPods Pro 3, verify feature availability in your region before purchasing.

Problems & Limitations to Know Before Buying

The AirPods Pro 3 are not perfect. The biggest concerns are:

  1. Static/Hiss Reports — Some users have reported unwanted background noise in ANC, Adaptive, and Transparency modes.
  2. Fit — The redesigned tips work very well for some people but may be uncomfortable for others.
  3. Battery Life — Up to 8 hours with ANC is useful, but some competitors offer stronger endurance.
  4. Apple Ecosystem Dependence — The AirPods Pro 3 make the most sense when paired with an iPhone.
  5. No Lossless Bluetooth Audio — Audiophiles looking for lossless or high-resolution Bluetooth codecs may prefer competing earbuds.

Final Verdict

The AirPods Pro 3 are a strong premium wireless earbud option, particularly for iPhone users.

Their biggest advantage is noise cancellation, which has received impressive results in independent testing. The addition of heart-rate sensing, fitness tracking, hearing health features, Live Translation, and IP57 resistance also gives them capabilities that go beyond simply playing music.

But they aren't perfect. The reported static and hissing issue deserves attention, the new ear-tip design won't work perfectly for everyone, and battery life isn't necessarily class-leading.

The ecosystem limitation is also significant.

If you're an iPhone user and can find the AirPods Pro 3 for around $189–$219, they become a particularly compelling purchase.

At the full $249, they're still strong, but it's worth comparing them with the latest alternatives before buying.

Bottom line: The AirPods Pro 3 are best suited to iPhone users who prioritize noise cancellation, Apple ecosystem features, fitness tracking, and hearing-related capabilities.
