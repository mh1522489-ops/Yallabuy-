What Is the AirPods 4 ANC Version — and How Is It Different from Standard AirPods 4?

The AirPods 4 launched in two variants that look nearly identical but perform differently. Both share the same redesigned, contoured shape and shorter stem, according to Apple's official product page. The key difference is that the ANC model adds Active Noise Cancellation, Adaptive Audio, Conversation Awareness, wireless charging, and a "Find My" speaker in the case — none of which are present on the standard $129 model.

This is also why the standard AirPods 4 can be priced so low relative to competitors: Apple positions it as an entry-level product without the H2 chip's full feature set, reserving noise cancellation and Personalized Spatial Audio for the $179 ANC version and above. In effect, Apple now sells four noise-and-feature tiers of AirPods (standard AirPods 4, AirPods 4 with ANC, AirPods Pro 2, AirPods Pro 3), each stepping up in ANC strength, battery life, and price, according to comparisons published by SoundGuys and Forbes.

Key Features

Redesigned Fit and H2 Chip

Apple says the AirPods 4 were rebuilt using a large database of ear scans to improve stability and all-day comfort, with a shorter stem and quick-press controls. The ANC model runs on the H2 chip — the same silicon used in the AirPods Pro 2 — which Apple credits for Adaptive Audio, Voice Isolation on calls, and hands-free Siri interaction.

Adaptive Audio and Transparency Mode

According to Apple and confirmed by hands-on coverage from HiFi Oasis, the AirPods 4 ANC offer four noise-control states: Off, Transparency, Adaptive Audio, and full Active Noise Cancellation, switchable via a long-press on the stem or through iOS settings. Adaptive Audio blends ANC and Transparency dynamically based on your surroundings. One independent reviewer at HiFi Oasis noted being "pleasantly surprised" that ANC blocked most home-office background noise like traffic and HVAC hum, even without a sealed fit — though sharp sounds like mechanical keyboard clicks came through more clearly.

Personalized Spatial Audio

The ANC model supports Personalized Spatial Audio with dynamic head tracking, a feature absent from the standard AirPods 4. Independent reviewer TechGearLab found this and Adaptive EQ to be useful additions but not dramatic upgrades to the listening experience.

IP54 Water and Sweat Resistance

Both the earbuds and the charging case are rated IP54, meaning resistance to dust and splashes/light rain — not full submersion, according to Apple's official specifications.

Battery Life: Real-World vs Apple's Claims

This is one area where manufacturer claims and independent lab tests genuinely disagree, so it's worth breaking down carefully.

Apple's official claim: up to 5 hours of listening with ANC off, and up to 4 hours with ANC on, per charge — with 20–30 hours total using the charging case, depending on whether ANC is active.

Independent testing (SoundGuys): In SoundGuys' lab testing, both the standard AirPods 4 and the AirPods 4 with ANC lasted 6 hours and 21 minutes of continuous playback with noise control switched off — notably longer than Apple's 5-hour figure. SoundGuys reports that enabling ANC or Transparency reduces this runtime, bringing it closer to Apple's stated 4–5 hour range, though it did not publish an exact ANC-on figure at time of testing.

How it compares to other Apple models: SoundGuys' independent testing put the AirPods Pro 2 at 5 hours 43 minutes with ANC on, and the newer AirPods Pro 3 at 8 hours 42 minutes with ANC on — a significant jump. That means the AirPods 4 ANC sits behind both Pro models for sustained ANC listening, even though its no-ANC runtime beats Apple's own official number.

Charging speed: According to SoundGuys' testing, five minutes in the case adds roughly one hour of playback, and a full charge takes about 90 minutes — figures that align with Apple's own claims.

User-reported experience: Some casual reviewers, including a contributor at HiFi Oasis, described the roughly 5-hour real-world battery life as "more than enough" for daily commuting and office use, while others (cited via a Tom's Guide long-term review referenced in later coverage) considered 5 hours underwhelming on its own, relying on the case for top-ups throughout the day.

ANC Performance: How It Compares to AirPods Pro 2 and Rivals

Because the AirPods 4 don't use ear tips, there was real skepticism about whether ANC could work at all in this form factor. The evidence suggests it works — but with clear limits.

Independent lab measurement (SoundGuys): SoundGuys' hybrid ANC testing found the AirPods 4 ANC cancel approximately 20 dB of noise in the mid and high frequencies — enough to meaningfully dampen chatter, traffic, and similar everyday noise — but the lowest bass frequencies, like engine rumble or HVAC hum, largely pass through untouched.

Comparison with AirPods Pro 2 and Pro 3

SoundGuys' side-by-side testing found the AirPods Pro 3 "easily outperforms" the AirPods 4 ANC in noise cancellation, and multiple outlets, including Forbes and SlashGear, note the AirPods Pro 2 also cancels more noise thanks to its silicone ear tip forming a physical seal — something the AirPods 4 simply cannot replicate. Forbes' comparison recommends the AirPods Pro 2 specifically for buyers who consider strong ANC a must-have, and the AirPods 4 ANC for value-conscious buyers who want "good enough" noise reduction.

Comparison with Third-Party Rivals (Sony, Bose, Samsung)

YallaBuy was unable to find independent lab tests directly pitting the AirPods 4 ANC against the Sony WH-1000XM5, Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds, or Samsung Galaxy Buds4 Pro using the same testing methodology, so we won't invent a numeric comparison. What can be said with confidence, based on general findings from SoundGuys' broader in-ear ANC testing across brands, is that sealed earbuds and over-ear headphones with cushioned ear cups (the categories Sony, Bose, and Samsung's flagship models fall into) consistently achieve deeper low-frequency noise cancellation than open-fit designs like the AirPods 4, simply because passive isolation from a physical seal does most of the work that ANC processing can't.

Editorial takeaway (YallaBuy analysis): The AirPods 4 ANC's noise cancellation is genuinely better than skeptics expected for a tip-free design, and reviewers across SoundGuys, What Hi-Fi?, and HiFi Oasis independently arrived at similar "better than expected, not class-leading" conclusions. Treat it as noise reduction for commuting and office use, not isolation for flights or loud environments.

Spatial Audio & Sound Quality

Independent testing from TechGearLab found the ANC model's sound signature emphasizes bass frequencies around 90 Hz more than the standard AirPods 4, producing a fuller sound, though the deepest bass and highest treble notes still sound comparatively "underwhelming" in their measurements. TechGearLab also noted that without a sealed ear tip, listeners can't achieve the kind of in-ear seal that produces truly immersive sound — a structural limitation of the design rather than a flaw specific to this generation.

Consumer Reports' initial hands-on impressions found the ANC version suppressed background noise noticeably better than the standard model while walking through New York streets and riding commuter trains, though the outlet deferred final sound-quality conclusions to its full lab testing process.

Personalized Spatial Audio with head tracking is available on the ANC model only. Reviewers generally describe it as a nice-to-have rather than a transformative feature for most listening.

How to Tell If Your AirPods 4 Have ANC

Because the two AirPods 4 models look almost identical, this is one of the most common points of confusion for buyers and secondhand shoppers. Based on Apple's own documentation, here's how to check:

1. Check the model number on the case. The ANC charging case model numbers are A3059 (and regional variants), while the standard model uses a different number — check the fine print on the inside of the case lid or in the original packaging.

2. Look for wireless charging support. Only the ANC version's case supports Qi and Apple Watch charger wireless charging. If your case charges wirelessly, it's the ANC model.

3. Check iOS Settings. Open Settings > Bluetooth, tap the "i" next to your AirPods, and look for a Noise Control option. If you see toggles for Off, Transparency, Adaptive, and Noise Cancellation, you have the ANC model. The standard AirPods 4 won't show this menu.

4. Long-press the stem. On the ANC model, a long press on the stem cycles through noise control modes (this can also be reconfigured to trigger Siri). On the standard model, this gesture only controls playback.

Is It Worth the Extra $50 Over Standard AirPods 4?

This comes down to how much you value noise cancellation and the H2-chip feature set.

Choose the ANC version if: you want any form of noise cancellation, use your earbuds for calls in noisy environments (Voice Isolation is ANC-exclusive), want wireless charging, or plan to use Personalized Spatial Audio.

Stick with the standard AirPods 4 if: budget is the main concern, you rarely need noise blocking, or you already own AirPods Pro 2/3 and just want a lightweight backup pair.

YallaBuy analysis: Given that the ANC model adds Adaptive Audio, Voice Isolation, Personalized Spatial Audio, and wireless charging — not noise cancellation alone — the extra $50 buys meaningfully more than a single feature. For most buyers choosing between the two AirPods 4 variants specifically, the ANC version is the better long-term value.

Final Verdict

The AirPods 4 with Active Noise Cancellation succeed at something that seemed unlikely on paper: bringing genuinely useful noise cancellation to an earbud with no ear tip seal. Independent testing from SoundGuys and What Hi-Fi? both describe the ANC performance as better than expected for an open design, even while acknowledging it falls short of the AirPods Pro 2, AirPods Pro 3, and sealed rivals from other brands.

For iPhone owners who want a lighter, cheaper alternative to the AirPods Pro line and don't need flight-cabin-level silence, the AirPods 4 ANC is a reasonable buy, especially when discounted. Buyers who prioritize best-in-class noise cancellation, longer battery life, or Android compatibility should look elsewhere — either at the AirPods Pro 2/3 or at sealed alternatives from other brands.
