What Makes the Roomba j7+ Stand Out

Things to Consider Before Buying the Roomba j7+

Floor Types in Your Home

The j7+ works best on hardwood, tile, and low to medium pile carpets. If your home has mostly high-pile rugs or thick carpeting, you may want to look at models with higher clearance and stronger carpet suction.

Pet Household Needs

If you have pets that occasionally have accidents, the obstacle avoidance is a genuine differentiator. For pet hair alone, many vacuums perform well — but the waste avoidance feature is harder to find elsewhere.

Clean Base Placement

The Clean Base needs a dedicated spot against a wall with at least 18 inches of clearance on each side. It's larger than a standard charging dock, so measure your space before committing.

Wi-Fi Requirements

The j7+ requires a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. If your router only broadcasts 5 GHz, or if your mesh system doesn't have a separate 2.4 GHz band, setup will be difficult. Check your network before purchasing.

Ongoing Costs

Budget for replacement bags (every 1–2 months depending on use), replacement filters (every 2 months), and eventually a new battery (every 1–2 years). These costs add up over time.
