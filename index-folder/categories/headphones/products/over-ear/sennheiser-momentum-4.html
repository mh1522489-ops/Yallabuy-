Sennheiser MOMENTUM 4 Wireless Review: Is It Still Worth Buying?

The Sennheiser MOMENTUM 4 Wireless is one of those headphones that refuses to disappear. Released in 2022, it entered a market dominated by products such as Sony's WH-1000X series and Bose's QuietComfort headphones. Instead of trying to win every category, Sennheiser took a different approach: prioritize sound quality, comfort, battery life, and flexible connectivity.

That strategy still makes the MOMENTUM 4 interesting several years later. But there is an important question in 2026: Does an older flagship-style headphone still make sense when newer models are available?

Quick Verdict

The Sennheiser MOMENTUM 4 Wireless remains a compelling over-ear ANC headphone for listeners who prioritize sound quality, battery life, comfort, and connectivity over having the newest features.

What Is the Sennheiser MOMENTUM 4 Wireless?

The MOMENTUM 4 Wireless is a premium-oriented pair of closed-back wireless over-ear headphones from Sennheiser.

The product is designed around several ideas:

  1. High-quality wireless audio
  2. Long battery life
  3. Adaptive active noise cancellation
  4. Comfortable long-duration listening
  5. Flexible wired and wireless connectivity
  6. Personalizable sound
  7. Travel-friendly portability

Unlike some headphones that are primarily designed around aggressive noise cancellation, Sennheiser has traditionally placed a strong emphasis on the actual listening experience. That distinction is important. A headphone can have excellent ANC while still producing an uninteresting or poorly balanced sound. The MOMENTUM 4 takes the opposite approach by making audio reproduction one of its central selling points.

Sennheiser uses 42 mm dynamic transducers and supports SBC, AAC, and aptX Adaptive codecs. The company also provides sound personalization and EQ controls through its Smart Control Plus application.

Design and Build Quality

The MOMENTUM 4 represents a major visual departure from the older MOMENTUM 3.

Instead of the distinctive metal-and-leather-inspired appearance of previous models, Sennheiser moved toward a simpler and more minimalist design. The result is not necessarily as visually distinctive. But there is a practical advantage. The design is relatively uncomplicated, with large ear cups, a padded headband, and folding mechanisms intended to make transportation easier.

The headphones weigh approximately 293 grams, according to Sennheiser's documentation. That is not exceptionally light for modern ANC headphones. However, weight alone does not determine comfort. The shape of the ear cups, padding, clamp force, headband pressure, and how the headphones distribute their weight all contribute to the actual experience. Independent testing from RTINGS describes the MOMENTUM 4 as comfortable and suitable for long listening sessions, including travel and office use.

Comfort

Comfort is one of the strongest reasons to consider the MOMENTUM 4. The large over-ear design allows the ear cups to surround the ears instead of pressing directly against them. This matters when listening for several hours.

For commuters, students, programmers, office workers, travelers, and people who spend much of the day listening to music or podcasts, comfort can be more important than having the lightest possible headphone. RTINGS specifically identifies the MOMENTUM 4 as a strong travel option because of its comfort and long battery life.

However, there is no universal fit. Head shape varies. Ear size varies. Hair, glasses, and the position of the headphones can also affect the acoustic seal. That means you should treat comfort assessments as general guidance rather than a guarantee that the headphones will fit every person perfectly.

Sound Quality

Sound is arguably the most important reason the MOMENTUM 4 remains relevant. Sennheiser gives the headphones a warm, bass-driven presentation, while still maintaining clarity through the midrange and treble.

RTINGS' measurements describe the default tuning as bassier than its target curve, while noting that the mids and treble remain relatively close to the reference. SoundGuys also describes the MOMENTUM 4's sound quality as one of its major strengths. This combination makes the headphones suitable for a wide range of mainstream music.

Bass Performance

Bass is one of the defining characteristics of the MOMENTUM 4. The low end has more emphasis than a neutral studio-style presentation.

That can be particularly enjoyable with:

  • Hip-hop
  • Electronic music
  • Pop
  • Rock
  • R&B
  • Cinematic music
  • Soundtracks

Bass-heavy tracks can sound full and energetic without requiring the listener to immediately open an EQ. However, listeners who prefer an extremely neutral presentation may find the default tuning warmer than they prefer. The important point is that the headphones provide EQ customization. Instead of accepting the factory tuning as permanent, users can adjust the sound through Sennheiser's software.

Midrange

The midrange is important for vocals, guitars, piano, dialogue, and many instruments. The MOMENTUM 4 generally maintains good clarity in this region. Vocals do not disappear simply because the headphones have emphasized bass. That makes the headphones useful for both music and spoken content.

For podcasts and YouTube videos, speech remains intelligible. For music, vocals can maintain presence while the lower frequencies provide additional weight.

Treble

The treble presentation is designed to complement the warmer sound rather than turn the MOMENTUM 4 into an aggressively bright headphone. This makes long listening sessions easier for people who are sensitive to overly sharp high frequencies.

Of course, treble preference is highly personal. Some listeners prefer a brighter presentation because it can create an impression of additional detail. Others prefer a smoother signature that does not become tiring during long sessions. The MOMENTUM 4 sits closer to the latter philosophy.

EQ and Sound Personalization

One of the advantages of modern Sennheiser headphones is that the listening experience is not limited to a single factory tuning. The Smart Control Plus application provides EQ and personalization options.

This is particularly useful because headphone preference is subjective. Two people can listen to the same headphones and prefer completely different tunings.

One person might want:

  • More bass
  • Less treble
  • Stronger vocals

while another might want:

  • Less bass
  • More upper-mid presence
  • More treble energy

The ability to customize the headphones means the MOMENTUM 4 can adapt to different preferences. Sennheiser also provides a Sound Personalization feature intended to tailor the listening experience to the listener's preferences.

Active Noise Cancellation

The MOMENTUM 4 uses hybrid adaptive active noise cancellation. The headphones use microphones to analyze environmental sound and generate an opposing signal designed to reduce unwanted noise.

This is particularly useful in environments such as:

  • Airplanes
  • Trains
  • Offices
  • Cafés
  • Public transportation
  • Busy streets
  • Shared workspaces

But ANC performance needs context. The MOMENTUM 4 does a good job with many everyday sounds, particularly voices and higher-frequency environmental noise. However, independent testing shows that it is not consistently the strongest option for very low-frequency rumbling.

RTINGS notes that the ANC performs well against conversational and higher-frequency sounds but is less effective with very low-frequency noise such as deep construction rumble. SoundGuys similarly describes the ANC as good but behind the strongest competitors.

That distinction is important. "Good ANC" does not mean "best ANC." To understand how ANC works and what makes it effective, read our guide on how active noise cancellation works.

ANC for Air Travel

Air travel is one of the most demanding environments for noise-cancelling headphones. Aircraft cabins contain a significant amount of persistent low-frequency noise.

The MOMENTUM 4 can reduce a substantial amount of environmental sound, but buyers whose number-one priority is airplane noise reduction should compare it directly with headphones known for particularly strong low-frequency ANC. If you're deciding between the top models, see our Sony vs Bose comparison.

The MOMENTUM 4's advantage is that ANC is only one part of the package. You also get:

  • Long battery life
  • Comfortable ear cups
  • Wired connectivity
  • USB-C
  • Strong music reproduction
  • Transparency mode

This makes it a versatile travel headphone even if ANC performance is not the absolute strongest available.

Transparency Mode

ANC is useful when you want to block the world out. Transparency mode is useful when you want to hear it again without removing the headphones. The MOMENTUM 4 provides an adjustable transparency/ambient listening mode.

This is useful for situations such as:

  • Hearing an airport announcement
  • Talking to a coworker
  • Ordering coffee
  • Crossing a busy environment
  • Listening for your surroundings

The feature makes the headphones more practical for everyday use.

Battery Life

Battery life is arguably the MOMENTUM 4's most impressive specification. Sennheiser rates it for up to 60 hours of music playback via Bluetooth with ANC enabled. That is a remarkably high figure for a premium ANC headphone.

More importantly, independent testing supports the idea that the battery is genuinely strong. SoundGuys measured 56 hours and 21 minutes in its standardized test with ANC enabled.

The exact result you experience will vary depending on:

  • Volume
  • Codec
  • ANC usage
  • Temperature
  • Bluetooth behavior
  • Firmware
  • Playback content
  • Battery age

So the manufacturer's 60-hour figure should be understood as a maximum rated figure rather than a guaranteed result for every user.

Why 60 Hours Matters

Battery capacity is not just a specification for the box. It changes how you use headphones. With a typical ANC headphone, charging becomes part of your routine. With the MOMENTUM 4, charging can become something you do much less frequently.

For example, a person who listens for approximately eight hours per day could potentially get several days of use from a single charge. For someone who listens for only a few hours each day, a full charge could last much longer. That makes the MOMENTUM 4 particularly attractive for travel.

Fast Charging

Sennheiser also supports fast charging. The company's current product information states that approximately five minutes of charging can provide up to four hours of playback under its specified conditions. The US product page also lists up to six hours from a 10-minute charge.

As always, real-world charging results depend on conditions. Still, the practical benefit is clear: You do not necessarily need to wait for a full charge before leaving home.

USB-C Connectivity

The MOMENTUM 4 is more flexible than a headphone that only supports Bluetooth. It can be connected using USB-C for audio. This can be useful with compatible computers and other devices.

Sennheiser also includes a traditional analog audio cable. That gives the headphones several possible connection methods:

  • Bluetooth → wireless listening
  • USB-C → digital wired audio
  • 3.5 mm → analog wired audio

This flexibility is particularly valuable for people who use their headphones across phones, tablets, laptops, and entertainment systems.

Bluetooth and Codecs

The MOMENTUM 4 supports Bluetooth 5.2. Supported codecs include:

  • SBC
  • AAC
  • aptX
  • aptX Adaptive

Sennheiser's documentation confirms aptX Adaptive support and Bluetooth 5.2 connectivity. aptX Adaptive is particularly interesting because it can dynamically adjust its behavior depending on the requirements of the connection.

However, codec support only matters when the source device also supports the relevant codec. Buying headphones with aptX Adaptive does not automatically mean every phone or computer will use it. The source device, operating system, Bluetooth implementation, and application all matter.

Multipoint Connectivity

The MOMENTUM 4 can maintain active connections with two Bluetooth devices. This is extremely useful for people who use headphones with both:

  • A phone
  • A laptop

For example, you could listen to music from a laptop and still have your phone connected for calls. This is one of those features that may seem minor before you use it regularly. Once it becomes part of your workflow, going back to headphones without multipoint can feel inconvenient.

Calls and Microphone Quality

The MOMENTUM 4 includes a microphone system designed for voice calls. Sennheiser specifies a four-microphone beamforming system, with microphones intended to improve voice pickup and reduce environmental interference.

In quiet environments, this can be perfectly adequate for:

  • Phone calls
  • Video meetings
  • Voice assistants
  • Casual communication

However, microphone performance is not the same as having a dedicated microphone. Independent testing has found that the microphone's ability to reject background noise can be a weakness. RTINGS describes the microphone system as passable and notes limitations in noise handling. SoundGuys also identified microphone noise rejection as an area where the headphones could improve.

Therefore, if your job depends on extremely clear voice communication in noisy environments, a dedicated microphone or a headset designed specifically around voice communication may be more appropriate.

Smart Controls

The MOMENTUM 4 uses touch controls rather than relying entirely on physical buttons. This allows common actions to be performed without reaching for the phone.

Depending on configuration and firmware, users can control functions such as:

  • Playback
  • Volume
  • Calls
  • ANC
  • Transparency
  • Other headphone functions

The controls can also be customized through the Sennheiser application.

Smart Pause and Auto On/Off

The headphones include automatic behaviors designed to make everyday use easier. Smart Pause can detect when the headphones are removed and pause playback. Auto On/Off can reduce the need to manually manage power.

These features sound small. But small conveniences accumulate. The less often you have to interact with your phone, the more natural the headphones feel during everyday use. However, automatic detection is not infallible. SoundGuys notes that its testing encountered situations where automatic shutoff behavior was inconvenient. For users who prefer complete control, manually managing power may be preferable.

The Sennheiser Smart Control Plus App

The app is an important part of the MOMENTUM 4 experience. Rather than treating the headphones as a fixed product, Sennheiser provides software controls that allow users to customize aspects of the listening experience.

Features include:

  • EQ
  • Sound modes
  • ANC controls
  • Transparency
  • Sound Personalization
  • Firmware updates
  • Control customization

The app effectively turns the headphones into a configurable audio platform.

Is the MOMENTUM 4 Good for Music?

Yes, music is one of its strongest use cases. The 42 mm drivers and warm tuning create a presentation that works particularly well with modern music. Bass-heavy genres benefit from the energetic low end, while vocals and instruments retain reasonable clarity.

The ability to customize the sound further improves flexibility. RTINGS' testing found very good audio reproduction accuracy overall, although the headphones have a bassier-than-target tuning. SoundGuys also highlights sound quality as one of the product's primary strengths.

Is the MOMENTUM 4 Good for Podcasts?

Yes. The over-ear design and clear midrange make the headphones well suited to spoken content. Podcasts, audiobooks, interviews, lectures, and YouTube videos do not require the same type of audio presentation as music, but the MOMENTUM 4 handles them comfortably. The large ear cups also help make long spoken-content sessions less tiring.

Is the MOMENTUM 4 Good for Movies?

The headphones can work well for movies and TV. The strong low end gives cinematic effects additional weight. The closed-back design also helps create a more isolated listening experience.

However, the MOMENTUM 4 does not provide the same specialized spatial-audio ecosystem offered by some newer headphones. Sennheiser's current product information does not list 3D Spatial Audio as an enabled feature for this model. If spatial audio is central to your buying decision, newer products may be worth investigating.

Is the MOMENTUM 4 Good for Gaming?

This is a more complicated question. The MOMENTUM 4 is not primarily a gaming headset. It does not offer the same console-oriented wireless connectivity you might find on dedicated gaming headphones.

RTINGS notes that its wireless connection is Bluetooth and that console compatibility is limited in the way expected from a general-purpose Bluetooth headphone. For PC gaming, wired connectivity can provide additional flexibility.

But if gaming is your primary purpose, a dedicated gaming headset may provide:

  • Lower latency
  • Better microphone integration
  • Console wireless support
  • Game/chat mixing
  • Gaming-specific software

The MOMENTUM 4 makes more sense as an all-purpose headphone that you occasionally use for gaming.

Is It Good for Working From Home?

For general office work, yes. The combination of comfort, ANC, long battery life, multipoint connectivity, and wired options makes it practical for desk use.

Imagine the following setup:

  • Your laptop is connected through Bluetooth.
  • Your phone is also connected.
  • You listen to music while working.
  • When a call arrives, you can switch to the phone.
  • The headphones can remain on your head throughout the day.

That is where the MOMENTUM 4's combination of features becomes more important than any individual specification. The main concern is microphone performance in noisy environments. RTINGS specifically identifies background-noise rejection as a weakness.

Is It Good for Travel?

Travel is one of the strongest use cases. The reasons are straightforward:

  • Long battery life
  • Comfortable fit
  • ANC
  • Transparency mode
  • Bluetooth
  • Analog cable
  • USB-C
  • Foldable design
  • Carrying case

Sennheiser includes a carrying case, USB-C cable, audio cable, and airplane adapter. That makes the package well suited to people who frequently travel.

Is It Good for the Office?

For many office environments, the MOMENTUM 4 makes sense. It can reduce distractions and provide music or background audio without requiring constant charging. Its adaptive ANC can reduce voices and other environmental sounds. RTINGS specifically describes it as suitable for office work, while noting that low-frequency noise and microphone performance have limitations.

Water and Sweat Resistance

This is an important limitation. The MOMENTUM 4 does not have an official water or sweat-resistance rating. That means it should not be treated like a sports headphone designed for exercise.

RTINGS notes the lack of sweatproofing, while SoundGuys lists the headphones as not waterproof. For walking and normal daily use, this is not necessarily a problem. For running, intense workouts, or frequent exposure to sweat and rain, you should consider a product designed for that environment.

That does not automatically make the MOMENTUM 4 obsolete. Older premium headphones can become more interesting when their market price falls significantly.

The key question becomes: How much are you paying for the older model compared with the newer generation?

If the difference is small, the newer product may provide a more compelling long-term proposition. If the MOMENTUM 4 is heavily discounted, its combination of sound, battery life, and features can become much more attractive.

Should You Buy the MOMENTUM 4 at Full Price?

This is where context matters. The MOMENTUM 4 launched as a premium product. But it is now an older generation. That changes the buying calculation.

If the price approaches that of newer premium headphones, you should compare generations carefully. If the MOMENTUM 4 is substantially discounted, its hardware becomes much easier to justify.

The important question is therefore not: "Is the MOMENTUM 4 a good headphone?" The more useful question is: "Is the MOMENTUM 4 a good headphone at today's price?" That distinction is central to a responsible buying decision.

Our Buying Advice

The MOMENTUM 4 is an excellent example of why product age and product quality are two different things. It is an older headphone. That is a fact. But age does not erase the strengths of the hardware.

The headphone still offers: 60-hour-class battery life. 42 mm dynamic drivers. aptX Adaptive. Bluetooth multipoint. USB-C audio. Analog audio. Adaptive ANC. EQ and sound personalization. Comfortable over-ear construction. Those are still useful features in 2026.

The bigger issue is price. If you find the MOMENTUM 4 at a substantial discount, the product becomes much more interesting. If it is priced close to newer flagship headphones, the calculation becomes different because you are giving up newer-generation features to save less money.

Final Verdict

The Sennheiser MOMENTUM 4 Wireless is not a headphone that needs to win every category to remain relevant. Its appeal comes from the combination.

You get excellent battery life, strong audio performance, comfortable over-ear listening, flexible connectivity, customizable sound, adaptive ANC, and useful travel features in one package.

Its weaknesses are equally important. The ANC is not the strongest in its class, the microphone is not ideal for noisy environments, the headphones lack an official water-resistance rating, and the product is now an older generation.

That makes the MOMENTUM 4 particularly sensitive to price. At the right price, it remains a very compelling headphone for music lovers, travelers, office workers, and anyone who values long battery life. But if you are paying near the price of a newer-generation model, compare both before buying.

The most useful way to think about the MOMENTUM 4 is not as "the best ANC headphones" or "the best headphones overall." Instead, it is a well-balanced premium headphone whose strongest advantages are sound, battery life, comfort, and versatility.
