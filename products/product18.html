If you are tired of cleaning your floors every day, a robot vacuum can make a big difference. But not every robot vacuum is smart enough to work well in a real home.

There are cables on the floor, shoes, socks, furniture, pet hair, and sometimes things that you simply forget to move before cleaning.

The iRobot Roomba j7+ (7550) was designed to deal with many of these everyday problems. It is a robot vacuum with smart mapping, object detection, automatic dirt disposal, and app control. It can also recognize some common objects and try to avoid them while cleaning.

But there is an important question:

Is the Roomba j7+ still a good choice today, or are you better off buying a newer robot vacuum?

In this review, we will look at its real strengths and weaknesses, who should buy it, who should avoid it, and what you should know before spending your money.

Roomba j7+ Quick Overview

The Roomba j7+ is a robot vacuum designed mainly for people who want automatic daily floor cleaning without constantly controlling the machine.

The biggest difference between the standard Roomba j7 and the j7+ is the included Clean Base automatic dirt disposal system.

Instead of manually emptying the robot's small dust bin after every few cleaning sessions, the j7+ can return to its base and automatically empty the dirt into a disposable bag.

iRobot says the Clean Base can hold up to 60 days of dirt and debris, although the real time will depend on how dirty your home is and how often you run the robot.

Main Features

  • Self-emptying Clean Base
  • PrecisionVision obstacle detection
  • Smart mapping
  • Room-by-room cleaning
  • App control (iRobot Home)
  • Alexa and Google Assistant compatibility
  • Automatic charging and resume cleaning
  • Dirt Detect technology
  • Dual multi-surface rubber brushes

  • Cliff detection

  • Lithium-ion battery

The robot itself measures about 13.3 inches wide × 3.4 inches high and weighs about 7.49 pounds.

What Makes the Roomba j7+ Different?

Robot vacuums have become much smarter than the simple models that randomly move around the house.

The Roomba j7+ is designed to understand more about its environment.

Instead of simply driving forward until it hits something, it uses sensors and PrecisionVision Navigation to identify and avoid certain objects.

This is especially useful in homes where the floor is not always perfectly clean before the robot starts.

For example, a charging cable on the floor can cause problems for many robot vacuums.

The Roomba j7+ is designed to recognize objects such as cables, socks, shoes, and pet waste and avoid them.

That does not mean the robot will recognize every object in every situation.

It is still a robot, and its obstacle detection should not be treated as a replacement for basic preparation.

However, this feature can make everyday cleaning easier.

1. Smart Mapping Is One of Its Best Features

One of the strongest reasons to consider the Roomba j7+ is its mapping system.

The Imprint Smart Mapping system allows the robot to learn the layout of your home.

After the robot has learned the rooms, you can use the app to choose specific rooms or areas that you want it to clean.

This is much better than simply pressing a button and hoping the robot eventually covers the whole house.

For example, imagine you have a kitchen, living room, bedroom, and office.

You can tell the robot to clean only the kitchen.

Or you can schedule certain rooms at certain times.

The system can also store maps for different levels of your home, which is useful if you have more than one floor.

For people who like automation, this can be one of the most useful features of the j7+.

2. Automatic Dirt Disposal Makes Daily Cleaning Easier

This is probably the biggest practical advantage of buying the j7+ instead of the regular j7.

The Clean Base automatically removes dirt from the robot's internal dust bin.

That means you do not have to manually empty the robot after every cleaning cycle.

The base uses enclosed dirt-disposal bags, and iRobot states that the bags can hold up to 60 days of dirt and debris under appropriate conditions.

This feature is especially useful for:

  • Busy people
  • Large homes
  • Families
  • Pet owners
  • People who run the vacuum every day
  • Anyone who dislikes handling dusty vacuum bins

However, remember that the "up to 60 days" figure is not a guarantee that every household will get two months from one bag.

A home with two dogs and a lot of carpet can fill the bag much faster than a small apartment with one person.

3. A Good Choice for Homes With Pets

Pet owners have a special problem with robot vacuums.

Dogs and cats can leave hair, food, toys, and other objects around the house.

The Roomba j7+ is designed with pet owners in mind.

Its dual multi-surface rubber brushes are designed to work across different floor types while helping reduce hair tangling. iRobot also gives the j7/j7+ its P.O.O.P. promise, which relates specifically to avoiding solid cat or dog waste under its stated terms and conditions.

The obstacle-detection system is particularly useful for pet owners because pet accidents are one of the worst things that can happen with a robot vacuum.

A normal robot vacuum could potentially drive through the mess and spread it around the floor.

The j7+ was specifically designed to reduce this risk.

Still, you should not assume that the robot is completely incapable of making mistakes.

Technology can fail, lighting and object position can affect detection, and no robot should be treated as 100% reliable around pet accidents.

4. Cleaning Performance

The Roomba j7+ uses a 3-stage cleaning system.

It combines its main cleaning brushes with an edge-sweeping brush and suction to collect dirt from different areas of the floor.

iRobot states that the j7+ offers 10× the Power-Lifting Suction compared with the Roomba 600 Series Cleaning System.

This comparison is important because "10× suction" does not mean that the j7+ is automatically ten times better than every modern robot vacuum.

It is a comparison against a specific older Roomba cleaning system.

In real-world use, the j7+ is designed for regular maintenance cleaning.

It can handle everyday dust, crumbs, dirt, and pet hair across hard floors and carpets.

But if your floor has very heavy debris, large particles, or deeply embedded dirt, a traditional vacuum can still be useful.

A robot vacuum should usually be viewed as a tool for maintaining cleanliness rather than completely replacing every type of vacuum cleaner.

5. Dirt Detect Technology

Another useful feature is Dirt Detect.

The robot can identify areas that need more cleaning attention and focus on dirtier sections of the floor.

This makes sense in areas such as:

  • Around the kitchen table
  • Near the entrance
  • Under dining tables
  • High-traffic areas
  • Around pet food areas

Instead of treating every part of the floor exactly the same, the robot can spend more attention where it detects more dirt.

This is a small feature compared with automatic emptying or smart mapping, but it can make the cleaning process more efficient.

6. Automatic Recharge and Resume

Large homes can create a problem for robot vacuums.

The robot may not have enough battery to clean the entire area in one session.

The Roomba j7+ can return to its base when it needs to recharge.

After charging, it can resume the cleaning job instead of simply stopping and waiting for you.

iRobot specifically describes the j7/j7+ as capable of returning to its base to recharge and then continuing where it left off.

This is important if you have a larger home.

You do not necessarily need the robot to finish everything in one battery cycle.

7. App and Voice Control

The Roomba j7+ is designed to work with the iRobot Home app.

The app allows you to control cleaning, schedule jobs, select rooms, and manage areas based on the map.

You can also use compatible voice assistants.

iRobot states that the j7+ works with voice assistants such as Alexa and Google Assistant.

This means you could potentially start cleaning without manually pressing a button on the robot.

For people who already have a smart home setup, this can make the j7+ more convenient.

Who Is the Roomba j7+ Suitable For?

The j7+ is designed to remove one of the most annoying chores from your day — but it is not for everyone. Here is who gets the most out of it.

Suitable for

  • Busy professionals who want the floors cleaned while they are at work
  • Pet owners with shedding dogs or cats
  • Families that generate more dust, crumbs, and hair than a single person
  • People with large or multi-floor homes who need recharge & resume
  • Smart-home users with Alexa or Google Assistant
  • Anyone who hates manually emptying a small vacuum bin
  • People who want a reliable, set-and-forget daily cleaning routine

Not suitable for

  • People who want a robot that also mops the floors
  • Buyers on a very tight budget looking for basic vacuuming
  • Residents of small apartments with very limited floor space for the Clean Base
  • Shoppers who specifically want the newest navigation and mopping features
  • Anyone expecting zero maintenance — brushes, filters, and sensors still need attention
  • People with extremely dark, glossy, or mirror-like floors where navigation sensors may struggle

Final Verdict: Is the Roomba j7+ Still Worth It?

The iRobot Roomba j7+ (7550) is a robot vacuum built around convenience.

Its biggest advantage is not simply that it can vacuum your floor.

The real advantage is how much of the process it can automate.

It can learn your home's layout, clean specific rooms, detect and avoid many common obstacles, automatically empty its dust bin, recharge itself, and continue cleaning.

For pet owners, the obstacle detection is particularly valuable.

For busy people, the scheduling and automatic disposal can make daily cleaning much easier.

But it is not perfect.

It does not mop, the Clean Base takes up space, replacement bags add an ongoing cost, and it is an older model compared with newer robot vacuums.

So should you buy it?

Yes, if you want a reliable, smart vacuum with self-emptying and you find the j7+ at a good price. No, if you want the newest technology, integrated mopping, or the lowest possible price.

The most important thing is not to buy it simply because it has a long list of features.

Buy it if those features solve problems you actually have.

For someone with pets, a busy schedule, and a home where cables and objects are often left on the floor, the Roomba j7+ can make everyday cleaning much easier.

For someone living in a small apartment who only needs basic vacuuming once or twice a week, it may be more technology than necessary.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about the iRobot Roomba j7+ (7550) — covering value, pet hair, mopping, mapping, and the Clean Base.

Is the iRobot Roomba j7+ still worth buying in 2026?

Yes — if you can find it at a good discount. The PrecisionVision obstacle detection, smart mapping, automatic dirt disposal, and pet-friendly features remain genuinely useful. The j7+ makes the most sense for pet owners and busy households that value set-and-forget daily cleaning. If you want the very latest technology, integrated mopping, or the lowest possible price, newer competitors may offer better value.

Does the Roomba j7+ mop floors?

No. The Roomba j7+ is a vacuum-only model — it does not mop. If mopping is important, look at the iRobot Roomba Combo j7+ or a competing robot vacuum that combines vacuuming and mopping in one unit.

How does the Roomba j7+ handle pet hair?

The j7+ uses dual multi-surface rubber brushes that resist hair tangling and work across hard floors and carpets. Combined with 10× Power-Lifting Suction (vs. the Roomba 600 series) and PrecisionVision obstacle detection, it is well suited for homes with shedding dogs and cats.

Can the Roomba j7+ avoid pet waste?

iRobot offers its P.O.O.P. (Pet Owner Official Promise) guarantee for the j7+. It is designed to detect and avoid solid pet waste under the stated terms and conditions. However, do not treat it as 100% reliable in every situation — lighting, object shape, and placement can affect detection.

How long does the Clean Base bag actually last?

iRobot states that the Clean Base can hold up to 60 days of dirt and debris under appropriate conditions. The real duration depends on household size, number of pets, floor type, and cleaning frequency. A two-dog household with lots of carpet can fill a bag much faster than a small one-person apartment.

Does the Roomba j7+ work with Alexa and Google Assistant?

Yes. According to iRobot, the Roomba j7+ is compatible with Alexa and Google Assistant. After setup through the iRobot Home app, you can start, stop, pause, or schedule cleanings using voice commands.

Can the Roomba j7+ save maps of multiple floors?

Yes. Imprint Smart Mapping can store maps for different levels of your home. You can label rooms, set keep-out zones, and ask the robot to clean specific rooms on demand through the iRobot Home app.

How big is the Clean Base and where should I place it?

According to iRobot, the Clean Base is roughly 15.7 inches wide × 11.9 inches deep × 13.3 inches high. Place it on a hard floor with enough clearance on each side and in front so the robot can dock reliably.
