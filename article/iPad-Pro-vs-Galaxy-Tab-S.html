iPad Pro M5 vs. Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra: Performance, Battery, Display & Value Compared

Both are premium tablets — but they prioritize very different things. Here's how they compare across benchmarks, battery, display, stylus, storage, and real value, using official specs and independent testing.

⚔️ Quick Comparison

The iPad Pro M5 and Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra are both premium tablets, but they emphasize different priorities.

The iPad Pro M5 has a substantial advantage in the major CPU and GPU benchmarks reviewed, while also offering a bright tandem-OLED display, Thunderbolt/USB4 connectivity, and Apple's established iPadOS ecosystem. Tom's Guide's direct testing against the newer Galaxy Tab S11 Ultra recorded 4,155 vs. 2,811 in Geekbench 6 single-core, 16,517 vs. 9,126 in multi-core, and 9,934 vs. 6,575 in 3DMark Wild Life Extreme Unlimited. The S11 Ultra uses a newer chip than the S10 Ultra, so these figures should not be presented as a direct S10 Ultra benchmark.

The Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra takes a different approach. Its 14.6-inch AMOLED display is considerably larger, the S Pen is included, the tablet has IP68 water and dust resistance, and Samsung supports microSD expansion up to 1.5TB on the model reviewed here.

There is also an important generation difference: Samsung has since released the Galaxy Tab S11 Ultra, so the S10 Ultra is no longer Samsung's current flagship tablet. That makes the S10 Ultra most interesting when its actual selling price is substantially below the newer model.

🖥️Display size: Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra.
☀️Display brightness and measured color accuracy: iPad Pro M5 has an advantage in Tom's Guide's testing.
🖊️Stylus included: Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra.
💾Expandable storage: Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra, with microSD support up to 1.5TB.
💧Water/dust resistance: Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra, IP68.
🔌Charging adapter included: iPad Pro M5 includes a 20W adapter; Tab S10 Ultra does not.
📅Software-support commitment: Samsung provides an explicit seven-year policy for the Tab S10 Ultra; Apple does not publish an equivalent fixed-term iPadOS guarantee.
🆕Current-generation Samsung alternative: Galaxy Tab S11 Ultra.

💵 Price & Availability

Prices change frequently, especially for an older flagship such as the Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra. Treat retailer prices as snapshots rather than permanent prices.

iPad Pro M5 pricing

Apple originally launched the M5 iPad Pro in October 2025 at $999 for the 11-inch Wi-Fi model and $1,299 for the 13-inch Wi-Fi model. Apple currently lists the 11-inch Wi-Fi model from $1,199 and the 13-inch Wi-Fi model from $1,499 in its U.S. store. Higher-storage configurations cost more, and 1TB/2TB models receive 16GB of unified memory rather than 12GB.

The current U.S. pricing is therefore the figure buyers should use when comparing the two products today, rather than relying on launch-price articles.

Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra pricing

The Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra launched in 2024 at premium-tablet pricing, but its position has changed since the arrival of the Galaxy Tab S11 Ultra in 2025.

Because the S10 Ultra is now an older-generation model, retailer discounts can materially change its value. However, discounts vary by retailer, storage configuration, country, and promotion period.

🔍 YallaBuy analysis: Don't evaluate the S10 Ultra using its original list price alone. Compare the actual price of the exact configuration you are buying with the current price of the Tab S11 Ultra. A large discount can make the older model considerably more competitive; a small discount makes the newer Samsung model more relevant.

🧭 What Makes Each Tablet Different?

The iPad Pro M5 is fundamentally a performance-focused premium tablet. Apple's M5 platform gives it desktop-class silicon performance in a very thin tablet, while iPadOS 26 provides improved window management and multitasking.

The Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra takes a different route: Samsung combines an unusually large 14.6-inch display with Android, Samsung DeX, an included S Pen, IP68 protection, and expandable storage.

Neither approach is automatically better for every user. The more useful question is which combination matches your actual workflow.

🖥️ Display

iPad Pro M5

The 13-inch iPad Pro M5 uses a Tandem OLED Ultra Retina XDR display with ProMotion from 10Hz to 120Hz, P3 wide color, True Tone, and up to 1,600 nits peak HDR brightness.

Tom's Guide measured 561 nits in SDR and 965 nits in HDR in its testing. Its measured color reproduction was also more natural than the Galaxy Tab S11 Ultra's, although that comparison is with Samsung's newer model rather than the S10 Ultra.

Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra

The Tab S10 Ultra has a 14.6-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X display at 2960 × 1848 resolution with up to a 120Hz refresh rate. Samsung also specifies anti-reflection treatment and HDR10+ support.

The biggest difference isn't simply image quality; it's physical size.

The Tab S10 Ultra gives you considerably more screen area for split-screen apps, large PDFs, handwriting, spreadsheets, and watching video.

🔍 YallaBuy analysis: If you frequently work with two apps side by side or want the largest possible tablet canvas, Samsung's 14.6-inch panel is a meaningful practical advantage. If display brightness, HDR performance, and more controlled color reproduction are higher priorities, the iPad Pro has stronger evidence behind it from independent testing.

⚡ Performance

This is the clearest measurable difference between the platforms.

Tom's Guide tested the iPad Pro M5 against the Galaxy Tab S11 Ultra, which uses Samsung's newer Dimensity 9400+. The results were:

These numbers demonstrate the M5's performance advantage over Samsung's newer flagship tablet in those particular tests. They should not be presented as direct benchmark results for the Tab S10 Ultra, because the S10 Ultra uses the older Dimensity 9300+.

That distinction is important for YallaBuy's evidence standard.

🔍 YallaBuy analysis: The benchmark gap matters most for demanding workloads such as video editing, 3D applications, advanced image processing, and some AI workloads. For web browsing, streaming, note-taking, office applications, and ordinary multitasking, the Tab S10 Ultra remains a high-performance tablet.

Raw benchmark performance should therefore be treated as a workload-dependent advantage rather than a guarantee that one tablet will feel faster in every everyday task.

🔋 Battery Life

Battery comparisons are particularly sensitive to testing methodology.

Apple rates the M5 iPad Pro for up to 10 hours of Wi-Fi web browsing or video playback under its specified test conditions. The 13-inch model has a 38.99Wh battery.

Tom's Guide recorded 13 hours 18 minutes for the iPad Pro M5 in its continuous Wi-Fi web-surfing test at 150 nits. The Galaxy Tab S11 Ultra lasted 12 hours 03 minutes in the same outlet's comparison. Again, this is a comparison with Samsung's newer S11 Ultra, not the S10 Ultra.

The Tab S10 Ultra has a much larger 11,200mAh battery, but battery capacity alone doesn't determine endurance. Display brightness, refresh rate, chipset efficiency, software behavior, workload, and network conditions all affect actual runtime.

Independent S10 Ultra results vary considerably because reviewers use different tests. Synthetic benchmarks, continuous video playback, web browsing, and intermittent real-world use cannot be treated as interchangeable measurements.

🔍 YallaBuy analysis: The safest conclusion is not that one tablet will always last longer. Instead, independent testing provides evidence that the iPad Pro platform can deliver very strong endurance despite its smaller battery, while the S10 Ultra's large battery does not automatically translate into proportionally longer runtime.

🔌 Does Either Tablet Include a Charger?

⚠️ This is an important correction from the earlier version of this article.
  • iPad Pro M5: Apple lists a USB-C charging cable and a 20W USB-C Power Adapter in the box.
  • Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra: Samsung's documentation states that the charger is not included and recommends a compatible 45W charger for optimal charging performance.

So the answer is:

The iPad Pro M5 includes a charger. The Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra does not.

Retail packaging can vary by market, so buyers should still check the exact regional listing before purchasing.

🖊️ S Pen vs. Apple Pencil Pro

The Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra includes an S Pen, making the stylus part of the standard tablet package. Samsung officially lists S Pen support for the device.

The Apple Pencil Pro is compatible with the iPad Pro M5 but is sold separately. Apple lists it at $129 in the U.S.

The difference is therefore straightforward:

  • Samsung: S Pen included.
  • Apple: Apple Pencil Pro is an additional purchase.

The better choice depends on the apps and workflow you intend to use. The iPad has access to applications such as Procreate and other established creative software, while Samsung's S Pen integrates closely with Samsung Notes, DeX, handwriting features, and the Android ecosystem.

🔍 YallaBuy analysis: If you know you'll use a stylus every day, the included S Pen reduces the effective purchase cost of the Samsung package. If you already rely on specific iPad creative applications, the Pencil Pro's separate cost may be less important than software compatibility.

🪟 Multitasking and Laptop Replacement

Neither tablet should be treated as a universal laptop replacement.

iPad Pro M5

iPadOS 26 has become more capable for multitasking, window management, and external-display workflows. Apple's M5 hardware can handle demanding applications, but iPadOS still differs from macOS in file management, software availability, background processes, and professional workflows.

The cost of accessories also matters. Apple's 13-inch Magic Keyboard is sold separately and is listed at $349 in the U.S.

Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra

Samsung DeX provides a desktop-style interface with resizable windows and mouse-and-keyboard support. It can make the large display particularly useful for productivity.

However, DeX is still an Android-based environment. Some professional desktop applications and workflows available on Windows or macOS are not replicated in the same way.

🔍 YallaBuy analysis: Don't buy either tablet as a "laptop replacement" based only on the marketing claim. Start with the applications you actually use.

If your workflow depends on specialized desktop software, check whether the exact application and features you need are available on the tablet before purchasing.

📅 Software Longevity

Samsung has a particularly clear policy here.

The Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra is included in Samsung's extended update program, with seven years of software and security support. Samsung's security-update scope currently lists the Tab S10 Ultra among supported Galaxy Tab S models.

Samsung's security updates for the Tab S10 series are delivered on a quarterly schedule rather than the monthly schedule used by some Galaxy smartphones.

Apple does not publish an equivalent fixed seven-year iPadOS commitment. Instead, the company has historically supported iPads for many years, but the exact future duration for a particular model is not guaranteed in a fixed-term policy.

🔍 YallaBuy analysis: Samsung offers a clearly documented support commitment, while Apple's strength here is its established historical record rather than a published fixed number of years. Those are different types of evidence and should not be presented as identical promises.

📷 Cameras, Speakers, and Build

The iPad Pro M5 has a 12MP rear camera and a 12MP landscape-oriented front camera designed around video calls and Center Stage-style framing.

The Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra uses 13MP + 8MP rear cameras and dual 12MP front cameras. Samsung's specification confirms the dual-camera setup and 4K video recording.

Neither tablet should be treated as a substitute for a modern flagship smartphone camera.

Size and weight

The 13-inch iPad Pro M5 weighs 579g in Wi-Fi configuration.

The Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra weighs about 718g in Wi-Fi configuration according to Samsung's published specifications.

That difference is noticeable, but the Samsung tablet also gives you a much larger 14.6-inch screen.

The Tab S10 Ultra additionally has an IP68 rating for water and dust resistance. Apple does not publish an IP rating for the iPad Pro M5.

📡 Connectivity and Expandable Storage

The iPad Pro M5 supports Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6 and uses a Thunderbolt/USB4 port. Apple also supports an external display up to 6K at 60Hz or 5K at 120Hz under its published specifications.

The Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra supports Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 5.3, with USB 3.2 Gen 1 connectivity and DisplayPort output. Samsung also officially lists microSD support up to 1.5TB.

This resolves an uncertainty in the earlier version of this article: there is no need to present microSD support as a disputed specification for the Tab S10 Ultra when Samsung's regional specifications explicitly list it.

🔍 YallaBuy analysis: Expandable storage is one of the clearest practical differences. Users working with large media libraries, downloaded video, or large files may value Samsung's microSD support.

🩺 Known Issues and Owner Feedback

YallaBuy separates recurring observations from isolated reports.

iPad Pro M5

  • Recurring reviewer observation: The hardware can be excessive for users whose needs are limited to browsing, streaming, basic note-taking, and ordinary productivity.
  • Recurring upgrade question: Reviewers have generally focused the M5 upgrade case on users coming from substantially older iPads or users with demanding workloads rather than treating the M5 as a necessary upgrade for every M4 owner.
  • Battery variation: Independent battery tests vary considerably depending on methodology, so individual results should not be treated as a guaranteed runtime.

Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra

  • Recurring observation: The Dimensity 9300+ provides strong performance but does not compete with the M5 in the major CPU/GPU benchmark comparisons available from the newer S11 Ultra.
  • Accessory issue: The S Pen is included, but the keyboard accessory is sold separately.
  • Charging: Samsung does not include the charger in the box, and optimal 45W charging requires a compatible charger sold separately.
  • No confirmed broad hardware-defect conclusion: We would not characterize isolated owner complaints as evidence of a widespread hardware failure without stronger independent evidence.

🆕 The Important Context: Galaxy Tab S11 Ultra

The Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra is no longer Samsung's current flagship tablet.

Samsung subsequently released the Galaxy Tab S11 Ultra, which uses the newer Dimensity 9400+ processor.

Tom's Guide's direct testing of the S11 Ultra against the iPad Pro M5 recorded the following:

  • Geekbench single-core: 2,811 vs. 4,155
  • Geekbench multi-core: 9,126 vs. 16,517
  • 3DMark Wild Life Extreme Unlimited: 6,575 vs. 9,934

The S11 Ultra therefore provides the more relevant current-generation Samsung comparison for the M5.

That does not make the S10 Ultra irrelevant. It simply changes the question.

Instead of asking: "Which flagship is newer?"

the buyer should ask: "How much less does the S10 Ultra cost compared with the newer S11 Ultra?"

If the price difference is small, the newer generation deserves consideration. If the S10 Ultra is heavily discounted, its large display, S Pen, IP68 rating, and expandable storage remain relevant advantages.

🎯 Who Should Buy Which?

Who Should Consider the iPad Pro M5?

The iPad Pro M5 makes the most sense for users who:

  • Work with demanding creative applications
  • Edit high-resolution video
  • Use professional illustration or music-production applications
  • Want maximum tablet-class CPU/GPU performance
  • Already use Macs, iPhones, or other Apple devices
  • Prioritize a high-brightness OLED display
  • Need Thunderbolt/USB4 connectivity
  • Prefer Apple's established iPadOS support history

Who Might Prefer a Different iPad?

The M5's hardware may be unnecessary if your main activities are:

  • Web browsing
  • YouTube and streaming
  • Reading
  • Basic note-taking
  • Email
  • Casual gaming
  • Simple productivity

In those cases, less expensive iPad models may provide enough performance without paying for the Pro hardware.

Who Should Consider the Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra?

The Tab S10 Ultra makes the most sense for users who:

  • Want a very large tablet display
  • Use split-screen applications frequently
  • Take handwritten notes
  • Want the S Pen included
  • Need microSD storage
  • Want IP68 water and dust resistance
  • Use Samsung DeX
  • Prefer Android and Samsung's ecosystem
  • Find the S10 Ultra at a substantially lower price than the newer S11 Ultra

Who Might Prefer the Galaxy Tab S11 Ultra Instead?

Consider the newer S11 Ultra when:

  • The S10 Ultra discount is small
  • You want the newer Samsung processor
  • You want a current-generation Samsung flagship
  • The price difference between the two models is relatively small

The S11 Ultra is the more direct current-generation Samsung comparison to the iPad Pro M5.

🔄 Alternatives Worth Considering

Apple iPad Air

The iPad Air provides many of the core iPadOS features at a lower price than the Pro line. For users who don't need the M5's maximum performance or Tandem OLED display, it can be a more appropriate fit.

Samsung Galaxy Tab S11 Ultra

This is the most obvious Samsung alternative to the S10 Ultra. It gives buyers a newer processor and current-generation hardware.

Samsung Galaxy Tab S10 FE+

The S10 FE+ is another option for buyers who want a large Samsung tablet but don't need flagship-level performance. Its lower position in Samsung's lineup also makes it worth considering when the main priorities are screen size, battery life, note-taking, and everyday productivity rather than maximum performance.

💰 Value for Money

Value depends heavily on the actual price at the time of purchase.

The iPad Pro M5 offers significantly more performance than most tablet users need, but that performance can be useful for demanding creative and professional applications.

The Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra offers a different value equation. Its large AMOLED display, included S Pen, IP68 rating, microSD support, and DeX make it a feature-rich package. Its main disadvantage today is that Samsung has already released a newer generation.

🏁 Final Takeaway

The iPad Pro M5 and Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra are better understood as two different approaches to a premium tablet rather than identical products competing on every specification.

The iPad Pro M5 offers the stronger benchmark performance in the independent testing reviewed here, a bright Tandem OLED display, Thunderbolt/USB4 connectivity, and Apple's established iPadOS ecosystem. Its main trade-offs are price and the additional cost of accessories such as Apple Pencil Pro and Magic Keyboard.

The Galaxy Tab S10 Ultra offers a much larger 14.6-inch AMOLED display, an included S Pen, IP68 protection, microSD expansion, and Samsung DeX. Its position is different today because the Galaxy Tab S11 Ultra has replaced it as Samsung's newer flagship tablet.

The most important factor when buying the S10 Ultra today is therefore its actual selling price relative to the S11 Ultra.

If the price difference is substantial, the S10 Ultra's feature set can still make sense. If the prices are close, the newer Samsung model becomes the more relevant comparison.

Rather than choosing based on a single "winner," match the tablet to your workload, preferred operating system, accessory needs, storage requirements, and actual price.
