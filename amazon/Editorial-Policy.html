Editorial Policy

How We Create, Review, and Maintain Content at YallaBuy

YallaBuy is committed to providing useful, clear, transparent, and trustworthy information that helps visitors make better decisions when shopping for electronics and technology products. This page explains how our content is researched, written, reviewed, and kept up to date.

Our Mission

Helping you understand products, not just buy them

The goal of YallaBuy is to provide useful information about electronics and technology products so visitors can make informed decisions. Our content is built to explain products clearly, not to push readers toward a purchase.

  • Product information
  • Product comparisons
  • Product reviews
  • Buying guides
  • Product features
  • Pros and cons
  • General technology advice
Our Process

How we create our content

Every article follows a consistent process designed to keep information clear, organized, and useful.

  1. Research the topic

    We start by understanding the product category and what readers need to know.

  2. Collect relevant information

    We gather product and technical details relevant to the topic.

  3. Compare available sources

    Information is compared across multiple relevant sources.

  4. Organize the information

    Key points are structured in a logical, easy-to-follow order.

  5. Write the article

    Content is written clearly and in plain, easy-to-understand language.

  6. Review for accuracy and clarity

    The draft is reviewed before it goes live.

  7. Publish the article

    Once reviewed, the article is published on the website.

  8. Update when needed

    Articles are revisited when important information changes.

Honest AI Usage

Use of artificial intelligence

YallaBuy may use artificial intelligence tools as a supporting technology during the content creation process. AI is not intended to replace human judgment or editorial responsibility.

Where AI may help

  • Organizing information
  • Structuring page content
  • Improving content organization
  • Helping with formatting
  • Generating ideas for headings
  • Assisting with basic research organization
  • Helping identify areas that may need clarification

What this does not mean

AI is not used as a substitute for editorial responsibility. Published content is reviewed and organized before publication, and editorial decisions remain the responsibility of our team.

We do not claim that every article is manually written from scratch, and we do not claim that every fact on this website is independently tested by YallaBuy.

Research

Product research

When researching a product, we may consider information such as:

  • Product specifications
  • Features
  • Customer feedback
  • Product ratings
  • Number of reviews
  • Price information
  • Availability
  • Manufacturer information
  • Relevant public sources

Prices, availability, ratings, and specifications can change over time, sometimes shortly after an article is published.

Reviews & Comparisons

Product reviews and comparisons

YallaBuy aims to provide balanced information. Where relevant, our reviews and comparisons discuss:

What we cover

  • Important features
  • Advantages
  • Disadvantages

Fit & suitability

  • Who the product may suit
  • Who may want alternatives
  • Key differences between products

Please note

We do not guarantee that a product will be suitable for every user, and we do not claim to personally test every product discussed on the website.

Affiliate Disclosure

How YallaBuy earns money

YallaBuy participates in affiliate marketing programs, including the Amazon Associates Program. This means that we may earn a commission when visitors purchase qualifying products through certain links on our website.

  • Affiliate links may generate a commission for YallaBuy.
  • The commission does not change the price you pay.
  • Affiliate relationships do not mean every product is automatically recommended.
  • We aim to provide useful and balanced information first.
Editorial Independence

Affiliate links do not control our content

Affiliate relationships do not mean that manufacturers or sellers control our editorial content.

  • We aim to present useful information objectively.
  • We do not guarantee positive coverage simply because a product has an affiliate link.
  • Product recommendations are based on relevance and usefulness to the reader.
Pricing & Availability

Pricing and availability can change

Product prices, discounts, availability, ratings, and specifications may change after an article is published. Please verify the current information on the retailer's website before making a purchase.

The retailer's current listing should be considered the final source for up-to-date price and availability.

Sources

Sources and information

YallaBuy may use information from a range of sources, including:

Content Updates

Content updates

Articles may be reviewed and updated when:

  • Product specifications change
  • Prices significantly change
  • Products become unavailable
  • Better alternatives become available
  • New information becomes available
  • Errors are discovered

Our goal is to keep important content useful and reasonably up to date. However, we cannot guarantee that every piece of information will always reflect real-time changes.

Corrections

Corrections

YallaBuy takes accuracy seriously. If an error is discovered, the affected content may be:

  • Corrected
  • Updated
  • Clarified
  • Removed when necessary

If you spot something that looks incorrect or outdated, we welcome you to let us know.

User Trust

Why transparency matters to us

Transparency is important to YallaBuy. We aim to:

  • Provide useful information
  • Clearly disclose affiliate relationships
  • Avoid intentionally misleading readers
  • Make content easier to understand
  • Improve content over time
