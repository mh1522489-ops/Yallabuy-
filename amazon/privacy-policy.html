Privacy at a Glance

  • No Accounts Needed: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide personal information to browse YallaBuy.
  • Affiliate Model: We direct you to Amazon via affiliate links. We do not sell products directly.
  • Newsletter: If you subscribe, your email is processed by MailerLite.
  • No Direct Tracking: We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixels, or directly set advertising cookies.
  • Third-Party Tools: We use specific third-party services for analytics and support, which have their own privacy practices.

Information We Handle

YallaBuy is designed to be accessible without mandatory data collection. There is no login, sign-up, membership system, or user profile required. You do not need to provide personal data simply to browse the website, search for products, or view comparisons.

Furthermore, YallaBuy does not feature user-generated content. There are no user reviews, comments, or areas where users can upload images or files.

Communicating with Us

YallaBuy does not use a contact form. However, you can contact us via Email, WhatsApp, or our Contact Page. If you choose to reach out, you may voluntarily provide personal information such as your name, email address, or any details you include in your message. We only process the information you explicitly provide to us during communication.

Newsletter

YallaBuy offers a newsletter subscription. If you choose to subscribe, the email address you provide will be handled by MailerLite, our third-party email marketing service provider.

MailerLite may process the data necessary to manage your subscription and send emails on our behalf. We do not invent or collect additional data beyond what is required for this service. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link included in our emails.

For more information on how MailerLite handles your data, please review the MailerLite Privacy Policy.

Analytics & Tracking

YallaBuy does not use Google Analytics. However, we use the following tools to understand website performance and user experience:

Google Search Console

We use Google Search Console as a tool to understand how our site performs in Google Search results. It is not an account system for users and does not require you to log in.

Contentsquare

YallaBuy uses Contentsquare to analyze website usage and visitor behavior. Contentsquare may collect or process information about how visitors interact with the website, which may include page interactions, clicks, scrolls, session behavior, and user experience analysis.

For more details, please see the Contentsquare Privacy Policy.

AI Chatbot

YallaBuy uses Chatbase to provide an AI chatbot within the website. If you interact with the chatbot, the messages or information you enter may be processed by Chatbase or its associated service providers. We do not store these conversations ourselves unless explicitly confirmed by the service configuration.

For more information, please review the Chatbase Privacy Policy.

Cookies & Local Storage

YallaBuy does not directly set its own essential, preference, session, or advertising cookies. We do not use a Cookie Consent Banner because we do not directly deploy cookies for tracking purposes.

However, certain parts of YallaBuy may use browser storage technologies such as localStorage to support website functionality. We do not use localStorage to store personal data unless explicitly required by a specific feature.

It is important to differentiate between Cookies, Local Storage, Service Workers, and third-party tracking technologies utilized by the external services we integrate.

PWA & Service Worker

YallaBuy supports Progressive Web App (PWA) functionality and uses a Service Worker. The Service Worker helps with caching, performance optimization, PWA functionality, and reliable loading of certain resources.

A Service Worker is not a cookie, does not collect personal data, and does not track users. YallaBuy currently does not use Push Notifications and does not request permissions for Location, Camera, Microphone, or Notifications.

Amazon Associates

Amazon Associates Disclosure: YallaBuy is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.

YallaBuy uses Amazon affiliate links. When you click a purchase link, you will be redirected to Amazon. YallaBuy does not sell products directly. We do not use the Amazon Product Advertising API. We may earn a commission from qualifying purchases.

Amazon may use cookies or other technologies to identify referrals and track qualifying purchases according to its own systems and policies. Once you leave YallaBuy and visit Amazon, your data processing is subject to the Amazon Privacy Notice and Amazon's Terms and Conditions.

Product Prices

YallaBuy displays information about product prices linked to Amazon. Please note:

  • Prices and availability are subject to change constantly.
  • Prices may change daily or at any time without prior notice.
  • The final price displayed on Amazon at the time of purchase is the price that applies to your transaction.
  • YallaBuy does not guarantee that the price displayed on our site will remain available or match the price on Amazon.

YallaBuy does not determine or control Amazon's pricing.

Third-Party Services

We rely on specific third-party services to operate YallaBuy. The known services currently used are:

  • Amazon (Affiliate Program)
  • MailerLite (Newsletter)
  • Contentsquare (Analytics)
  • Chatbase (AI Chatbot)
  • Vercel (Hosting & CDN)
  • Google Search Console (Search Performance)

We do not use services such as Google Analytics, Cloudflare, Mailchimp, SendGrid, Facebook Pixel, TikTok Pixel, Hotjar, Google Ads, or AdSense.

Hosting & CDN

YallaBuy is hosted on Vercel, which provides our infrastructure and Content Delivery Network (CDN). Vercel may process technical information necessary to deliver and secure the website, such as request and connection data, in accordance with its own policies.

For more information, please see the Vercel Privacy Policy.

HTTPS & Security

YallaBuy uses HTTPS/TLS to help protect data as it travels between your browser and our website.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or loss. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Data Sharing

YallaBuy does not sell personal data. We do not maintain a user database or an account system. However, information may be processed by the necessary third-party service providers required to run the website (such as Vercel, MailerLite, Contentsquare, Chatbase, and Amazon), according to the purpose of each service and its specific policies.

External Links

YallaBuy contains links to external websites, particularly Amazon. When you leave YallaBuy and visit an external site, your data processing becomes subject to the privacy policy and terms of that external website.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer period is required or permitted by applicable law.

When it comes to third-party providers, retention periods may vary according to their specific policies and configurations.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and the laws that apply to you, you may have certain privacy rights. Where applicable, these rights may include:

  • The right to access your personal information.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate data.
  • The right to request deletion of your data.
  • The right to restrict processing.
  • The right to object to processing.
  • The right to withdraw consent.
  • The right to data portability, where applicable.

Where applicable, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws, including laws such as the GDPR or applicable U.S. state privacy laws.

Children's Privacy

YallaBuy is not directed toward children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the website.

International Visitors

While YallaBuy is primarily aimed at users in the United States, it is globally accessible. Users from different countries can access the site. Please note that some of our service providers may process information in different countries. Privacy laws and your rights may vary depending on your location.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

YallaBuy may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
