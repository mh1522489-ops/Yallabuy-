Keeping your home clean can take a lot of time. Vacuuming, mopping, dealing with dust, and handling pet hair can quickly become part of your daily routine.

The ECOVACS DEEBOT X2 COMBO is designed for buyers who want to automate that routine as much as possible.

It is not simply a small robot vacuum that moves around the house. It combines a robot vacuum, a robot mop, an OMNI cleaning station, and a cordless handheld vacuum into one complete cleaning system.

One of its most interesting selling points is that it includes the handheld vacuum directly inside the OMNI station, so the system can automatically empty both the robot and the handheld unit, wash the mop pads with hot water, dry them with hot air, refill water, and recharge the battery.

But there is an important question:

Does all of this actually make the X2 COMBO a good buy at $310?

The answer is more interesting than a simple "yes."

The DEEBOT X2 COMBO is one of the more complete all-in-one cleaning packages you can buy in this price range, but it also has trade-offs you should understand before spending your money.

In this detailed review, we will look at its cleaning performance, OMNI station, mopping system, navigation, handheld vacuum, advantages, disadvantages, price, and who should — and should not — buy it.

What Is the ECOVACS DEEBOT X2 COMBO?

The ECOVACS DEEBOT X2 COMBO is an all-in-one home cleaning system.

The main robot is designed to vacuum and mop your floors automatically. It uses advanced navigation technology to move around your home, create maps, detect obstacles, and clean different areas.

The system also includes an OMNI station. This station is responsible for many of the maintenance tasks that normally require manual work.

After cleaning, the robot can return to the station, where the system can empty the dust, wash the mop pads, dry them, refill the robot with water, and recharge the battery.

The COMBO version also includes a cordless handheld vacuum.

That makes the X2 COMBO particularly attractive for people who want their robot to become a regular part of their cleaning routine instead of something they need to manually maintain every day.

8,700Pa Suction: Is It Really That Powerful?

One of the first things you will notice when looking at the X2 COMBO is the 8,700Pa suction rating.

That is a strong number for a robot vacuum in this price range.

It can help collect:

  • Dust
  • Dirt
  • Crumbs
  • Hair
  • Pet hair
  • Food particles
  • Small debris
  • Everyday floor dirt

But there is something important every buyer should understand:

Pa is not the same thing as real-world cleaning performance.

A robot vacuum with a higher Pa number is not automatically better than another robot with a lower number.

Brush design, airflow, roller design, navigation, cleaning passes, floor type, and software all influence how much dirt the robot actually collects.

For the buyer, the important takeaway is simple:

The DEEBOT X2 COMBO has the hardware to handle everyday dust, hair, crumbs, and pet hair across most hard floors and low- to medium-pile carpets.

Mopping Performance and the 15mm Mop Lift

The DEEBOT X2 COMBO is also a mop.

It mops hard floors using rotating mop pads that apply cleaning action while the robot moves around the house.

This can be useful for everyday dirt such as:

  • Food marks
  • Dust
  • Light stains
  • Footprints
  • Kitchen dirt
  • Dried water marks

The system is particularly useful in homes with mostly hard floors.

One of the most useful features is the 15mm intelligent mop lift.

When the robot detects carpet, it raises the mop pads to prevent wet pads from remaining in contact with the carpet.

This is especially useful in homes with a combination of hard floors and carpet. Instead of manually removing the mop pads every time the robot moves toward carpet, the system raises them automatically.

For daily maintenance on hard floors, the mopping system performs well, but it does not fully replace occasional manual deep cleaning for very stubborn stains.

The OMNI Station: Where Most of the Magic Happens

The OMNI station is one of the most important parts of the X2 COMBO system.

A basic robot vacuum usually requires you to empty the dustbin manually. A basic robot mop also requires you to wash the mop pads yourself.

The X2 COMBO tries to automate all of this.

After completing a cleaning cycle, the robot can return to the OMNI station, where several automatic functions are available.

The station can:

  • Empty the robot's dust
  • Wash the mop pads with hot water
  • Dry the mop pads with hot air
  • Refill the robot's water
  • Recharge the robot
  • Store the handheld vacuum
  • Empty the handheld vacuum's dust

This significantly reduces the amount of daily maintenance required.

Instead of finishing a cleaning session and immediately cleaning the robot yourself, you can allow the station to handle most of the routine work.

Of course, the system is not completely maintenance-free. You will still need to clean the station, replace consumables, empty or refill tanks when required, and occasionally check the brushes and filters.

But compared with a traditional robot vacuum, the X2 COMBO can dramatically reduce the amount of work you need to do.

Hot Water Mop Washing

The OMNI station can use heated water to clean the mop pads.

Hot water can help remove dirt and residue from the pads more effectively than using cold water alone.

This is particularly useful if you use the mopping function regularly.

For example, if you run the robot every day after dinner, the robot can return to the station afterward and have its mop pads cleaned automatically.

That means you do not have to remove dirty mop pads after every cleaning session.

Regular mop washing can also help keep the cleaning pads in better condition over time.

However, you should still inspect and clean the mopping system periodically because automated washing does not eliminate the need for occasional manual maintenance.

Hot-Air Mop Drying

After washing the mop pads, the station can also dry them using hot air.

This is an important feature because leaving wet mop pads sitting inside a closed station for long periods can create unpleasant odors and even mildew.

The hot-air drying system helps reduce the moisture left on the pads after cleaning.

This makes the X2 COMBO more convenient for people who use the mop frequently.

It is one of those features that may not seem important when you first look at the product, but becomes much more useful when you use the robot every day.

Dual-Laser LiDAR Navigation

Navigation is one of the most important factors in a robot vacuum.

The X2 COMBO uses Dual-Laser LiDAR technology to help understand its environment and navigate around the home.

LiDAR can help the robot identify walls, furniture, rooms, and other objects while building a detailed map of the home.

This allows the robot to follow planned cleaning routes instead of simply moving randomly around the room.

Better navigation also helps reduce unnecessary movement and makes cleaning more organized.

This is particularly useful in larger homes where random navigation would take longer and potentially miss areas.

The robot can also save multi-floor maps, set no-go zones, and target specific rooms for cleaning through the app.

AIVI 3D Obstacle Avoidance

The X2 COMBO also uses AIVI 3D obstacle avoidance technology.

This system is designed to help the robot identify and avoid objects in its path.

That can be useful when your floor contains objects such as:

  • Shoes
  • Toys
  • Furniture
  • Cables
  • Pet-related objects
  • Small household items

However, no robot vacuum should be considered completely immune to obstacles.

For the best cleaning results, it is still recommended to remove loose cables, clothing, and very small objects from the floor before starting a cleaning session.

The better your floor is prepared, the easier it is for almost any robot vacuum to work effectively.

Slim Square Robot Design

The DEEBOT X2 COMBO uses a square-shaped design instead of the traditional round shape found on many robot vacuums.

This design is intended to help the robot clean closer to corners and edges.

The robot is also relatively low, which can help it reach under some beds, sofas, tables, and other furniture.

This can be useful because dust and hair often collect underneath furniture where traditional vacuums may be difficult to use.

The square design also gives the robot a different approach to edge cleaning compared with conventional circular robot vacuums.

The Handheld Vacuum Is a Major Advantage

The word "COMBO" is important.

The X2 COMBO does not only include a robot vacuum and mop. It also includes a cordless handheld vacuum.

This makes the system much more versatile.

A robot vacuum is excellent for floors, but there are many areas where a robot simply cannot work.

The handheld vacuum can be used for:

Sofas

You can use it to clean crumbs, dust, hair, and other debris from sofas and chairs.

Beds

The handheld vacuum can help clean mattresses and other fabric surfaces.

Stairs

Robot vacuums generally cannot clean stairs, so a handheld vacuum provides a convenient solution.

Furniture

You can use the handheld vacuum on desks, shelves, cabinets, and other furniture.

Tight Areas

Crevice attachments can help reach narrow areas where the robot cannot fit.

The handheld vacuum is stored and emptied inside the OMNI station, so it charges automatically along with the rest of the system.

This is the single biggest difference between a normal robot vacuum and the X2 COMBO.

Battery, App, and Voice Control

The DEEBOT X2 COMBO uses a 6,400mAh lithium-ion battery.

Battery life depends on the cleaning mode and conditions.

Using higher suction levels generally consumes more battery, while quieter modes can provide longer operating times.

The robot's ability to automatically return to the OMNI station is also important. If the robot needs additional charging during a large cleaning job, it can return, recharge, and continue cleaning when appropriate.

The X2 COMBO is controlled through the ECOVACS HOME app. The app gives you access to cleaning schedules, rooms, maps, cleaning modes, vacuum settings, mopping settings, and robot status.

The robot also supports voice control through ECOVACS' YIKO assistant and is compatible with common smart-home platforms. So instead of opening the app every time, you can use voice commands for supported functions.

Homes with mostly hard floors

What Maintenance Does It Still Need?

Despite the impressive automation, you should not expect the DEEBOT X2 COMBO to maintain itself forever.

You will still need to:

  1. Empty or replace the dust bag in the OMNI station
  2. Refill clean water
  3. Empty dirty water
  4. Clean the dock washing area
  5. Check and clean the main brush
  6. Remove trapped hair
  7. Replace filters when needed
  8. Replace mop pads when worn out
  9. Keep sensors clean for accurate navigation

The exact frequency depends heavily on your home.

A house with two dogs will obviously require more maintenance than a small apartment occupied by one person.

The important point is that the X2 COMBO reduces maintenance dramatically — it does not eliminate it.

Final Verdict: Should You Buy the ECOVACS DEEBOT X2 COMBO?

The ECOVACS DEEBOT X2 COMBO is a strong choice for buyers who want a single system that handles vacuuming, mopping, and handheld cleaning — without buying three separate devices.

Its biggest strengths are easy to understand:

8,700Pa suction, smart mopping with 15mm mop lift, an OMNI station that empties dust, washes and dries mop pads, and refills water, Dual-Laser LiDAR navigation, AIVI 3D obstacle avoidance, a slim square design, and a real cordless handheld vacuum that lives inside the station.

The system also has weaknesses.

The OMNI station is large, the overall system is more complex than a basic robot vacuum, and the handheld vacuum is not a replacement for a full-size cordless if you regularly deep-clean large areas of thick carpet.

That means we would not recommend buying it blindly.

But if you have a home with mostly hard floors, want automatic daily cleaning, and can find the X2 COMBO at the $310 price, it becomes a very compelling option.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about the ECOVACS DEEBOT X2 COMBO — based on the official spec sheet and how the system performs in real homes.

Is the ECOVACS DEEBOT X2 COMBO worth $310?

At $310, the DEEBOT X2 COMBO offers strong value if you want a single system that combines a robot vacuum, a robot mop, an OMNI station with automatic dust emptying, hot-water mop washing and hot-air drying, plus a cordless handheld vacuum. If you only need a basic robot vacuum, a simpler and cheaper model will be enough.

How strong is the 8,700Pa suction on the X2 COMBO?

8,700Pa is a high suction level for a robot vacuum. It is more than enough to handle everyday dust, crumbs, hair, pet hair, and small debris on hard floors and low-pile carpets. Final cleaning performance also depends on brush design, airflow, and floor type.

What does the OMNI station actually do?

The OMNI station automatically empties the robot's dustbin, washes the mop pads with hot water, dries the pads with hot air, refills the robot's water tank, recharges the battery, stores the handheld vacuum, and empties the handheld vacuum's dust. This removes most of the routine maintenance associated with robot vacuums and mops.

Does the DEEBOT X2 COMBO work on carpets?

Yes, but the X2 COMBO is best suited for hard floors and low- to medium-pile carpets. The 15mm mop lift raises the mop pads when carpet is detected to keep the carpet dry. For very thick or high-pile carpets, compare the X2 COMBO with a model that is specifically optimized for deep carpet cleaning.

What is the included handheld vacuum used for?

The cordless handheld vacuum is designed for areas that a robot cannot easily reach, including sofas, cushions, beds, stairs, desks, shelves, cabinets, cars, and tight corners. It is stored and emptied inside the OMNI station, so it charges automatically along with the rest of the system.

How well does the mopping system work?

The mopping system is designed for everyday maintenance on hard floors such as tile, vinyl, laminate, and wood. The 15mm intelligent mop lift prevents the wet pads from touching carpets. For heavy, stuck-on grime you will still want a manual deep clean, but for daily upkeep it reduces the amount of hands-on mopping you need to do.

Is the DEEBOT X2 COMBO noisy?

Like most robot vacuums, noise depends on the cleaning mode. Lower suction levels are quieter, while maximum suction and the auto-empty cycle at the station are louder. The handheld vacuum is comparable in volume to a standard cordless stick vacuum.

Do I still need a regular vacuum if I buy this?

For most homes, the DEEBOT X2 COMBO removes the need for a daily manual vacuum. However, the handheld vacuum is not a replacement for a full-size cordless or upright vacuum if you regularly deep-clean large areas of thick carpet or do heavy-duty cleaning jobs.
