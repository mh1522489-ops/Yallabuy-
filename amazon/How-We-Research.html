How We Research

Before we publish any product recommendation, comparison, or buying guide, we spend time researching. This page explains exactly how that process works, where our information comes from, and what we do not do.

Our Research Process

Every piece of content on YallaBuy goes through a straightforward research process. We do not have a complicated lab or testing facility. Instead, we focus on gathering information from multiple sources and organizing it in a way that helps readers make decisions.

  1. 1

    Understand the user need

    We start by identifying what problem the reader is trying to solve. This could be finding a budget phone, choosing between two laptops, or understanding which TV features actually matter for a specific room size.

  2. 2

    Identify relevant products

    We look for products that could realistically address that need. We do not limit ourselves to the most popular or most advertised options. A less-known product might be a better fit for a particular use case.

  3. 3

    Gather information from multiple sources

    We collect specifications, features, pricing, and user feedback from the sources described below. We try to cross-check important details across more than one source.

  4. 4

    Analyze and compare

    We compare products based on the information gathered. We look at how each product performs for the specific need we identified, not just which one has the highest specs on paper.

  5. 5

    Write and publish

    We organize our findings into clear, useful content. We explain our reasoning so readers can decide for themselves whether a recommendation fits their situation.

Where Our Information Comes From

YallaBuy does not generate product information from thin air. We rely on a combination of sources, each serving a different purpose in our research.

Official Manufacturer Websites

This is our primary source for product specifications, features, compatibility details, and official product descriptions. When we need to confirm a technical specification, the manufacturer's own website is the first place we check.

Amazon

We use Amazon to check product availability, current pricing, customer ratings, and most importantly, customer reviews. Reading what real buyers say about a product after using it is one of the most valuable parts of our research.

Independent Reviews and Testing

When available, we refer to trusted independent sources that perform hands-on testing, such as RTINGS and other reputable review sites. Independent test data helps us understand real-world performance that specs alone cannot show.

Social Media and User Discussions

We sometimes look at social media discussions to understand real user experiences, common complaints, and what people are saying about a product in everyday conversation. However, social media posts are not automatically treated as factual evidence.

A note about AI tools

We may use AI as a work assistant to help organize information or draft content. However, we do not treat AI-generated information as a verified source. Any important product fact is checked against reliable sources, especially the manufacturer's official website and other trustworthy references. We do not claim something is true simply because an AI model said so.

How We Verify Information

Not everything published online is correct. Product specifications can be misstated, features can be misunderstood, and claims can be exaggerated. We try to reduce these errors through a simple verification approach.

Cross-checking important claims

When we encounter an important product claim or specification, we look for confirmation from more than one reliable source. For example, if a product's battery life is stated differently on two websites, we will check the manufacturer's official specifications to find the most authoritative number.

When sources disagree

If information conflicts between sources, we do not simply pick the answer that makes a product look better. We research the claim further, give more weight to the most authoritative source (usually the manufacturer for specs), and if we cannot determine which information is correct, we do not present uncertain information as a confirmed fact.

What we cannot verify

We are honest about the limits of our research. We cannot guarantee that every single specification or detail in every article is correct at all times. Product information can change, mistakes can happen, and we encourage readers to check the manufacturer's website for the most current specifications before making a purchase decision.

How We Evaluate Products

Our product evaluations are guided by one main question:

Does this product actually solve a problem for the reader?

We do not simply choose products because they are famous, expensive, popular, or heavily marketed. A product might have impressive specifications on paper but fail to deliver value for a typical user. Another product might be less known but offer a much better combination of features, performance, and price for a specific situation.

When we recommend a product, it is because we believe it provides a useful combination of features, performance, price, reliability, or overall value for a particular use case. We try to explain why we think a product is a good fit so readers can judge for themselves.

Our evaluations are based primarily on the information we gather during research: manufacturer specifications, independent test results where available, and patterns we observe in customer experiences. We do not have a scoring formula or a rigid point system. Each product is evaluated in context.

How We Use Customer Reviews

Customer reviews, especially on Amazon, are an important part of our research. They provide insight that specifications and marketing materials cannot.

What we look for

  • Common strengths across multiple reviews
  • Recurring complaints or problems
  • Reliability concerns mentioned by multiple users
  • Comfort, usability, or setup issues
  • Features users actually appreciate in practice

What we do not do

  • Treat one individual review as absolute proof
  • Copy reviews and present them as our own
  • Assume every negative review is a real defect
  • Copy the Amazon star rating as our own
  • Ignore suspicious reviews that could skew results

About YallaBuy's own product ratings

When we give a product our own assessment or rating, it is based on our analysis of user experiences and the information collected during research. Our rating is not simply copied from Amazon, and the Amazon star rating should not be treated as the same thing as YallaBuy's evaluation. They are separate assessments.

We Do Not Personally Test Every Product

We want to be completely clear about this: YallaBuy does not personally test the products it covers. We do not have a testing lab, and we do not claim hands-on experience with products we have not actually used.

You will never see us say "we tested this product" or "our testing showed" unless we actually performed that test. We do not invent test results, and we do not imply personal experience when we do not have it.

Instead, we are transparent about where our information comes from. When we discuss a product's performance, we clearly distinguish between:

Manufacturer claims

What the brand says about its own product, such as battery life estimates or display brightness numbers.

Independent testing

Results from trusted third-party sources that have actually measured or tested the product hands-on.

Customer experiences

What real buyers report after using the product in their daily lives, including both positive and negative feedback.

YallaBuy's analysis

Our interpretation of all the above, organized to help readers understand how a product might fit their needs.

This approach means our content is based on research and analysis, not personal hands-on testing. We believe being honest about this is more useful to readers than pretending otherwise.

How We Handle Prices

Product prices, especially on Amazon, can change frequently. A price that is accurate today may be different tomorrow.

When we display a price on YallaBuy, it represents the price that was available at the time we researched or wrote the article. It is not a guaranteed price, and we cannot promise it will remain the same when you visit the retailer.

We encourage readers to always check the current price before making a purchase. The price you see on YallaBuy may be higher or lower than what is actually available at the time you are reading.

When we mention that a product is a good value, that assessment is based on the price we observed during our research. Value can change as prices fluctuate.

Affiliate Disclosure

YallaBuy uses Amazon affiliate links. This means that if you click on a link to a product on Amazon and make a purchase, YallaBuy may earn a commission from that sale.

This is how we sustain the website and continue creating content. However, affiliate commissions do not determine which products we recommend, how we rank them, or what we write about them.

Our product selections are made based on research and analysis, not on how much commission a product might generate. A product that pays a higher commission will not rank above a product that we believe is genuinely better for the reader.

We would rather recommend the right product and keep our readers' trust than push a higher-paying affiliate link and lose credibility. Our priority is helping you make a better buying decision.

As an Amazon Associate, YallaBuy earns from qualifying purchases. This does not affect the price you pay. Amazon affiliate links are clearly identifiable as they direct you to amazon.com or your local Amazon storefront.

How We Keep Content Updated

Electronics products change frequently. New models are released, specifications are updated, and prices shift. We try to keep our content relevant, but we want to be realistic about what that means.

We update product information when we become aware of important changes, such as a new generation of a product replacing an older one, significant specification changes, or when a product is discontinued.

We do not claim that every article is monitored or updated every day. Some articles may contain information that was accurate at the time of writing but has since changed. If you notice something that seems outdated, we appreciate you letting us know.

Where possible, we include the date when an article was last updated so readers can gauge how current the information is.

What We Do Not Do

Transparency is not just about explaining what we do. It is also about being clear about what we do not do. Here is a list of things you will not find on YallaBuy.

  • We do not invent test results or claim to have tested products we have not actually tested.
  • We do not treat AI-generated information as a verified or authoritative source.
  • We do not copy customer reviews and present them as our own personal experiences.
  • We do not recommend products simply because they are popular or heavily marketed.
  • We do not choose or rank products based on how much affiliate commission they generate.
  • We do not present uncertain information as a confirmed fact.
  • We do not guarantee that every piece of information in every article is always correct.
  • We do not claim that YallaBuy's rating is the same as the Amazon star rating.
  • We do not present ourselves as a laboratory, testing company, or professional product-testing organization.

YallaBuy cares about giving readers accurate and useful information, not just making a sale.

If you have questions about our research process or notice something in our content that needs correction, we welcome you to reach out. Being useful to readers is what matters most to us.
