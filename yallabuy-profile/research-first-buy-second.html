    The Research Journey

    How YallaBuy researches products.

    YallaBuy does not display a product and immediately suggest buying it. There is a sequence — six stages — that turns scattered information into a usable decision.

    01 — Stage

    Discover

    Identify products within a category and map the landscape before focusing on specifics.

    02 — Stage

    Research

    Gather information from multiple distinct source types — not only manufacturer claims.

    03 — Stage

    Compare

    Place products against each other using consistent criteria, not marketing narratives.

    04 — Stage

    Analyze

    Identify patterns, trade-offs, and what specifications actually mean in real use.

    05 — Stage

    Explain

    Write the findings in plain language, with context — not jargon.

    06 — Stage

    Decide

    Present the decision in terms of fit — who the product is for, and who it isn't.

    Principles

    What makes YallaBuy different.

    YallaBuy isn't defined by having more products or faster reviews. It's defined by how it treats information — and by what it refuses to do.

    01 / Principle

    Transparency

    We clearly distinguish between manufacturer information, independent testing, owner feedback, and YallaBuy's own analysis. Every type of source has its own weight, and we mark which is which.

    02 / Principle

    No Fake Testing

    We don't claim to personally test products when we haven't. If our coverage is based on documentation, specs, or owner feedback rather than hands-on testing, we say so openly.

    03 / Principle

    Evidence First

    Product information should be supported by reliable sources whenever possible. Where a claim can't be sourced, we either label it as analysis or leave it out — we don't dress it up as fact.

    04 / Principle

    Buyer Context

    A product isn't simply good or bad. What matters is whether it fits the user's needs. YallaBuy frames products around who they're for — and who they're not for.

    Information Sources

    Six types of evidence.

    YallaBuy doesn't rely on a single kind of information. Each source type is treated differently — and labeled so readers know what they're looking at.

    Honest
    A Commitment

    We don't claim
    what we didn't do.

    If we didn't personally test a product, we tell you. If a claim is based on documentation rather than hands-on use, we label it. If we don't know something, we leave it out rather than invent it.

    Transparency is more important than pretending.

    What's been built so far.

    YallaBuy is not a finished product — it's an ongoing project. Below are the milestones that have shaped the platform as it exists today.

    2026
    Milestone 01

    YallaBuy launched

    Initial public release of the platform, establishing the editorial direction and core commitment to product research over product promotion.

    Foundational Release
    Milestone 02

    Product research system

    Established the internal framework for how products are researched, structured, and presented — including the six-stage research journey that still guides coverage today.

    Milestone 03

    Product category architecture

    Defined how products are organized across categories, ensuring consistent comparison criteria and avoiding the混乱 of unstructured catalogues.

    Milestone 04

    Deals system

    Introduced a deals layer that surfaces pricing information without letting it override the research-first sequence. Price is presented after context, not before.

    Milestone 05

    Insights system

    Added an editorial layer for broader product guidance — category-level writing that sits above individual product pages and explains how a market works.

    Milestone 06

    Editorial framework

    Formalized the writing standards that keep coverage consistent — distinguishing manufacturer claims, independent findings, owner feedback, and YallaBuy's own analysis.

    Milestone 07

    Performance improvements

    Continuous work on speed, accessibility, and rendering — because a research platform that loads slowly or works inconsistently isn't usable as a research platform.

    Milestone 08 — Ongoing

    Continuous development

    YallaBuy is not a finished product. It continues to evolve across discovery, research, comparisons, deals, insights, editorial systems, and user experience.
